# R18: Ljung-Box Power on Binary Streams

R18 establishes a global positive control bounding what the manuscript's Ljung-Box non-rejections exclude at L278, L290, L286, and L318. The instrument attains 80% power against a lag-1 autocorrelation of 0.0511 at n = 8000, lag 20, level 0.05; a non-rejection therefore excludes ρ₁ > 0.0511 with probability 0.8. Measured autocorrelations on the Figure 6 classifier-error stream (max |ρ₁| = 0.0008) and the R11 sign stream (max |ρ₁| = 0.0007) lie far below this bound, with instrument power ≈ 0.050 at both values. The non-centrality parameter at 80% power is constant (20.96) across horizons, confirming θ₈₀ follows an n⁻¹ᐟ² law.

## Method and Validation

The analytic power uses a non-central χ² approximation against a symmetric two-state Markov chain with ρ(k) = (2θ)^k. Empirical power matches the analytic curve within 0.0421 on the domain power_analytic < 0.95 against a tolerance of 0.0474 (three standard errors). Size at the null (n = 8000) is 4.5% with Wilson [3.4%, 6.0%], and KS calibration against Uniform(0,1) yields p = 0.214. Cluster-bootstrap intervals cover the analytic root at all horizons except n = 32000 (flagged as a finding, probability 0.1855 under the null).

## Key Numerics

The detectable amplitude grid estimate is θ₈₀ = 0.0253, ρ₈₀ = 0.0506 at n = 8000. The analytic root is θ₈₀ = 0.0256, ρ₈₀ = 0.0511. Design effect is 1.96 on 36000 paired readings. All 24 tests pass including the self-invalidating assertion that all measured |ρ₁| < ρ₈₀.
