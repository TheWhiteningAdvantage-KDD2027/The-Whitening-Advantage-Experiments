# R08 — The Adverse Direction and the Discrete Null Law

R08 establishes the two qualifications of the manuscript's claim that the Concept threshold is exact. First, injected centring bias moves the false-alarm rate in both directions according to its sign at identical whiteness loss (L311, Figure 8 Panels A–B). Over-centering collapses the false-alarm rate to near-zero (0.95% at b = 0.15 for the biased arm), while naive under-centering inflates it to approximately 21% for the reference arm. Both arms yield identical Ljung-Box whiteness loss across the bias grid, measured as maximum |delta_lb_pp| = 2.21 points at b = 0.075, corroborating the adverse direction mechanism.

Second, the attainable levels under the CUSUM dead band are discrete: with δ = 0.1, the two-sided increments move by +2δ and -3δ, placing M_H on a 2δ = 0.2 lattice (L241, Figure 8 Panel C). The nearest attainable level at or below the nominal 5% is λ* = 11.4, with bracketing levels of 5.08% at λ = 11.2 and 4.32% at λ = 11.4. The operator delta between the weak (M >= λ*) and strict (M > λ*) operators is 0.76 points, quantifying the floating-point boundary effect.
