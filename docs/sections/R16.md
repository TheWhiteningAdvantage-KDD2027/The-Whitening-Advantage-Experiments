# R16 — Regime census and sign floor

R16 carries the most-cited empirical claim of the submitted manuscript: 80% of dated directional episodes fall out of budget. It dates the bull and bear phases of SPY, PFF, VNQ and BWX over 2000-2025 with a multi-scale Pagan-Sossounov and Lunde-Timmermann filter, computes the Sharpe ceiling ADD_min >= 504 ln(1/alpha)/SR^2 for each phase, and reports what fraction the ceiling excludes at gamma in {20, 252, 1260}. Every published value reproduces exactly: 66 phases (SPY 30, PFF 7, VNQ 18, BWX 11), 53 of 66 out of budget at gamma = 20 (80.3%), 52 on the exactly-priced sign arm, 64 at gamma = 252 (97.0%), with the two numerals of L260 closed forms matching to machine precision.

## Execution

`./run_experiment_R16.sh` executes the two-stage chain: _a dates the phases and writes the census, _b prices the two detection floors, the feasibility grid and the LaTeX macros. No figure is rendered; v87's census paragraphs reference only fig:oracle_frontier from R01.

## Expected artefacts

The canonical 66-phase configuration is the repository baseline; counterfactual arms (strict_ps: 48 phases, symmetric: 102 phases) are measured, persisted and reported but gate nothing.

## Known deviations

Five entries are registered in docs/DEVIATIONS.md. R16-dating-misdescription (L329, Class A, D3): a Pagan-Sossounov dating of all four streams yields 48 phases, not 66; the 66 require substituting Lunde-Timmermann on SPY. R16-floor-frac-envelope (L329, Class A, D2): the 55-92% envelope measures [50.1%, 92.1%] over the 13 detectable phases. Three entries carry no severity: boundary-sensitivity, sign-arm-disagreement and substitution-scope.
