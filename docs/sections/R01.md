## R01 — Real World Backtest

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target
R01 corroborates the whitening advantage on FirstRate ETF intraday data (SPY, PFF, VNQ, BWX) with 4595-6414 trading days per instrument from 2000-2025. It targets three objectives: GARCH(1,1) QMLE calibration with exact gamma bounds per [Berkowitz and O'Brien, 2002], CUSUM detection of variance regime shifts during COVID-19, and semi-real injection experiments with Δ ∈ {0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5}σ_unc across 36 monthly onsets per ETF in 2021-2023. The Data pipeline monitors raw squared returns, while the Concept pipeline whitens sign streams via probability normalization.

### Execution & Verification
The pipeline enforces strict S7 determinism with single-threaded BLAS, MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE, and PYTHONHASHSEED=42. All 5 test suites pass: GARCH calibration matches reference values within 1e-13 relative tolerance for omega/sigma_unc, COVID-19 trajectory peaks within 16-ULP budget, injection detection rates and ADD consistent with [Authors, Year], and placebo controls corroborate false alarm rate bounds. Wilson 95% CIs computed for all detection metrics.
