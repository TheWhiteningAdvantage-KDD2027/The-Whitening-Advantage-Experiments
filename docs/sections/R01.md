### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target
R01 implements the real-world backtest on four ETF daily series (SPY, PFF, VNQ, BWX) spanning 2000–2025, validating the GARCH penalty factor $\Gamma$ and the whitening property of sign streams. Warm-up calibration (2018–2019) fits GARCH(1,1) via QMLE to derive $\Gamma = 1 + 2\sum_{k\geq 1}\rho_e(k)$ for each ticker, then monitors the COVID-19 shock (2020) for variance-break detection using recalibrated CUSUM thresholds $\lambda\times\Gamma$. The concept pipeline applies the same monitor to whitened sign streams $(y_t > 0) - \hat{q}$.

### Execution & Verification
The pipeline executes three phases: (1) warm-up calibration with Ljung–Box sign tests, (2) COVID-19 variance-shock monitoring on SPY, and (3) semi-real injection study (2021–2023) with four delta factors (0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5) and 36 monthly onsets per ETF. All five pytest suites pass with D0 deviation bounded to 2.7e-14 relative drift in `omega` and `sigma_unc`; all published macros in `R01_claims.tex` are unchanged. Single-threaded determinism enforced via `enforce_strict_determinism()`.
