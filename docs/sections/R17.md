# R17 — Estimation Cost of the Parametric Route

R17 quantifies the estimation cost of the parametric route to threshold calibration (v87 L341): a symmetric GARCH(1,1) with α+β = 0.85 fitted by Gaussian QMLE on finite warm-up windows, compared against the sign route which estimates nothing. With n = 250, the QMLE persistence collapses to median α̂+β̂ = 0.63, delivering FPR 10.5% (vs. 9.5% in v87); the level returns by n = 500 with Wilson interval covering 5%. The sign arm shows no warm-up dependence: WLS slope on log(n_warmup) +0.0021 with paired-bootstrap interval [−0.0153, +0.0195], p = 0.84. No figure of v87 is rendered; the witness PNG is vendored under data/reference/R17/.

## Methodology

Protocols 3a–3d simulate GJR-GARCH(1,1) at α = 0.05, β = 0.80, ν = 7 with 128-bit role-and-index seeding. Protocol 3d sweeps n_warmup ∈ {250, 500, 1000, 2000} against γ_lev ∈ {0.00, 0.28}, 200 streams per cell, monitors squared standardized residuals with one-sided Strict CUSUM (δ = 0.5, threshold = 65). Sign arm monitors (ε_t > 0) − 0.5 at fixed dead band and threshold. Both arms run under --qmle-options specs (compliant with specification §1.10) and --qmle-options legacy (diagnostic, certifies no v87 value). All ten controls C1–C9 pass.
