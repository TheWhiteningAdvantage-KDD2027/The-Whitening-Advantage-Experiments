# R17 — Estimation cost of the parametric route

v87 opposes two routes to a calibrated threshold. The **parametric route** estimates the GARCH
parameters by QMLE on a warm-up window, standardizes the stream with the fitted parameters, and
monitors the standardized residual. The **sign route** estimates nothing: the binary error stream of
a non-anticipative sign classifier is exactly i.i.d. `Bernoulli(1/2)` whatever the volatility
dynamics, so its threshold is exact. R17 prices what the first route costs when the warm-up is
finite — the persistence it recovers, the false-alarm rate it delivers, and the window length at
which the level returns — and measures the second on the same axis.

R17 is the stream that measures **directly** what R04 established indirectly, and the two are
cross-read in the log.

**This stream renders no figure of v87.** `grep -c 'Fig10_Econometric_Baseline'` on the frozen
manuscript returns `0`. The witness PNG is vendored under `data/reference/R17/` and declared
**produced and not cited**, on the footing `AUDIT_R03.md` gives `R03_sensitivity.csv`. Nothing under
`results/R17_econometric_baseline/` is a figure, and the directory carries no `figures/`.

## Execution

```bash
chmod +x run_experiment_R17.sh
./run_experiment_R17.sh
```

The orchestrator runs the script twice: the **protocol specification §1.10 arm**, which certifies v87, and then the
**`--qmle-options legacy` attribution arm**, which certifies nothing and exists to separate "the
stopping criterion moved the number" from "the port broke the number". The second run is
unconditional and comes after the first: a diagnostic run only when a result looks wrong is an
instrument of selection. `PYTHONHASHSEED=42` is exported by the shell and verified by the script,
which exits if it is unset. The suite is the remit of `run_tests.sh` and this script never calls
`pytest`.

Measured cost, on the machine of record, single-threaded: **362.2 s** for the SPECS arm and
**338.6 s** for the legacy arm, 700.8 s in all. The submitted campaign ran the same four protocols
in 332 s without the per-fit diagnostics, the paired bootstrap or the option pricing.

## What v87 publishes, and what this campaign measures

L341, in full:

> *Estimation cost.* Eco-L1 must fit a persistent GARCH (`α+β = 0.85`) on a finite warm-up: on a
> `250`-step window the estimated persistence collapses to a median `α̂+β̂ = 0.62` and the FPR
> nearly doubles to `9.5 %`; the level is restored from `n = 500` onward. The asymmetry is
> structural: the sample median is asymptotically `N(0, 1/(4n f_z(0)²))` with *no* finite-moment
> requirement, while QMLE normality requires a finite fourth moment (`κ_z < ∞`) and its variance
> explodes under financial heavy tails. The sign pipeline is warm-up-independent in practice
> (measured FPR `3`–`8 %` across all warm-up lengths).

| v87 numeral                          | source cell                                     | witness   | regenerated     | class |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | --------- | --------------- | ----- |
| true persistence `0.85`              | `ALPHA_DGP + BETA_DGP`, by design               | `0.85`    | `0.85`          | D0    |
| median `α̂+β̂ = 0.62` at `n = 250`     | `persistence_median_pooled`, cell `(250, 0.00)` | `0.6212`  | **`0.6258`**    | D2    |
| FPR `9.5 %` at `n = 250`             | `FPR_Eco`, same cell                            | `9.5 %`   | **`10.5 %`**    | D2    |
| level restored, `3.0 %` at `n = 500` | `FPR_Eco`, cell `(500, 0.00)`                   | `3.0 %`   | **`7.0 %`**     | D2    |
| sign FPR `3`–`8 %`                   | `FPR_ML`, min–max over the four warm-up lengths | `3`–`8 %` | **`10`–`11 %`** | D2    |

**The asymptotic variance of the median is not measured here and no macro carries it.**
`1/(4n f_z(0)²)` is an analytic result L341 cites to van der Vaart; this stream measures neither the
innovation density at zero nor the sampling variance of a median.

**Every qualitative claim of L341 holds.** The persistence collapses at `n = 250` and climbs back
with the window (`0.626`, `0.775`, `0.841`); the parametric false-alarm rate falls monotonically
with the warm-up on both leverage settings, with no inversion beyond two paired standard errors, and
its `n = 500` Wilson interval covers the nominal level on both; and the sign arm's rate carries a
weighted least-squares slope on `log(n_warmup)` whose paired-bootstrap interval covers zero. The
displacement of the numerals is registered as `R17-campaign-redraw`.

## The measurement

`protocol_3d` simulates a GJR-GARCH(1,1) stream at `α = 0.05`, `β = 0.80` — persistence `0.85`,
which is L341's own figure — with standardized Student-`t7` innovations, fits a **symmetric**
GARCH(1,1) by Gaussian QMLE with variance targeting on the first `n_warmup` steps, freezes the fit,
filters the next `5000` steps forward with it, and runs a one-sided strict CUSUM on the standardized
squared residual. The sign arm monitors `(ε_t > 0) − 0.5` at a fixed dead band and a fixed
threshold, and reads no fitted and no true parameter. The sweep is `n_warmup ∈ {250, 500, 1000,
2000}` against `gamma_lev ∈ {0.00, 0.28}`, 200 streams per cell.

### The convergence flag does not measure convergence

The delivered flag is `res.success and max(|a − 0.05|, |b − 0.90|) > 1e-6`. It detects an SLSQP
failure or a return to the initialiser, and **nothing else**. A corner solution at the optimiser's
lower bound `(1e-6, 1e-6)` — persistence about zero, no GARCH at all — is recorded as *converged*.
The submitted table exhibits it: at `(250, 0.00)` it carries `share_nonconverged = 0.0` while its own
`alpha_hat_10 = 1.0000000042e-06` and `beta_hat_10 = 1.0000000000e-06` sit on the bound.

R17 carries the flag byte for byte — it is the estimator's behaviour and a measured quantity, not an
infrastructure failure, so §S4.3 is discharged by **recording** every fallback rather than by halting
on one — and derives three further flags *outside* the primitive, per fit, so that its `ast` identity
is untouched:

| cell `(n_warmup, γ_lev)` | `share_nonconverged` | `share_at_lower_bound` | median `α̂+β̂` |
| ------------------------ | -------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------ |
| `(250, 0.00)`            | `0.000`              | **`0.290`**            | `0.6258`     |
| `(500, 0.00)`            | `0.000`              | `0.180`                | `0.7748`     |
| `(1000, 0.00)`           | `0.000`              | `0.060`                | `0.8406`     |
| `(2000, 0.00)`           | `0.000`              | `0.010`                | `0.8392`     |
| `(250, 0.28)`            | `0.015`              | `0.010`                | `0.9608`     |
| `(500, 0.28)`            | `0.010`              | `0.005`                | `0.9665`     |
| `(1000, 0.28)`           | `0.005`              | `0.000`                | `0.9717`     |
| `(2000, 0.28)`           | `0.000`              | `0.000`                | `0.9758`     |

**Twenty-nine per cent of the fits behind the published median are corner solutions the flag calls
converged.** The share falls monotonically with the window, which is what makes it a finite-warm-up
artefact of the estimator rather than a coding defect. A third of the persistence collapse L341 reports 
is therefore driven by this optimiser boundary, not just by finite-sample variance. It is measured, 
persisted per fit and asserted in the test suite; it opens no register entry, because v87 prints no 
convergence share and §S8 admits only a formal contradiction of a printed claim. Candidate 
`docs/camera_ready_candidates/R17_v87_persistence_collapse_mechanism.md` clarifies this mechanism.

### The published median, declared before the regenerated values were read

`\RSeventeenMedianPersistenceAtWarmupTwoFifty` is **the median of the per-fit sum `α̂ + β̂` over all
fits, converged and not**, at `(250, 0.00)`. L341 reads "the estimated persistence collapses to a
median `α̂+β̂`", which is the median *of the sum*, and pooling is what a practitioner obtains. The
rule was logged before the campaign ran.

The witness stores marginal medians and never the median of the sum: its `0.62` is
`alpha_hat_50 + beta_hat_50 = 0.047881 + 0.573349 = 0.621230`. Both constructions are computed on
the same fits and persisted side by side on both arms, which decomposes the gap into three **named**
terms instead of one residual:

| term                                                         | value     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------- |
| total gap against v87's `0.62`                               | `+0.0058` |
| definitional (median of the sum − sum of marginal medians)   | `+0.0427` |
| optimiser options (SPECS arm − legacy arm, on a common draw) | `−0.0001` |
| 128-bit redraw (residual)                                    | `−0.0368` |

The definitional term is the largest of the three, and it is the one that does not depend on any
draw: **on the same fits, the two constructions differ by four points of persistence.**

### The sign arm: one arm, and a degeneracy asserted rather than remarked

Both simulators draw the whole innovation vector **before** the variance recursion, and every
conditional variance is strictly positive, so `sign(ε_t) = sign(z_t)` **exactly** and the monitored
binary stream is a function of the entropy key, of `ν` and of `n` alone — of no process parameter.
Under the mandated key, which carries role and index only:

- protocol 3a: `n = 7000` fixed, so the stream is **bit-identical at all eight `Γ`** and the `ML`
  column is one measurement printed eight times. The submitted campaign prints the same constant
  (`0.065`) for the same reason under its own integer seeds.
- protocol 3b at `c = 0` and protocol 3c: the same identity, on their own grids.
- protocol 3d: the key omits `gamma_lev`, so the stream is **bit-identical at the two leverage
  settings** for each warm-up. The submitted table exhibits it directly — `FPR_ML` is equal at the
  two settings for every warm-up length (`0.075/0.075`, `0.030/0.030`, `0.055/0.055`, `0.080/0.080`).
  Along `n_warmup` the vector length changes, so the four evaluation windows overlap by 65 % to 95 %
  but are **not** identical: that axis carries four genuine draws, and it is the axis L341 makes its
  claim about.

The identity is **asserted** by SHA-256 over the monitored streams and the run exits `1` otherwise,
so a later change to either simulator cannot break the statement silently. Eight cells therefore
hold four readings; a mean over the eight carries a Kish design effect of exactly `2.0`.

**The `3`–`8 %` envelope is published with its effective count declared and gates nothing.** It is a
min–max over four readings of 200 streams each, and preamble §S4bis's fourth corollary is explicit
that an extremum over a grid has no stable sampling distribution. Furthermore, **the tight `10`–`11 %` 
regenerated envelope is an artefact of pairing**: because `n_warmup` is excluded from the seed, 
the four cells share their innovation vector and differ only by the evaluation window shift. 
Independent samples would span ~5 points. It ships with a paired stream bootstrap: `[5.5, 13.0] %` 
around the minimum, `[7.5, 15.5] %` around the maximum. The invariance L341 asserts is tested 
instead by a statistic that **has** a distribution — a weighted least-squares slope of the rate 
on `log(n_warmup)`, weights from the binomial variance at 200 streams and not from the residuals, 
with its null law taken from the same paired bootstrap so that the overlap of the windows is priced: 
slope `+0.0021` per natural log unit, interval `[−0.0153, +0.0195]`, `p = 0.84`. A four-cell homogeneity 
`χ²` is reported beside it (`0.261` on 3 df) with its dependence stated rather than assumed away.

**Control design under §S4bis.** Every multi-test control logs its family-wise arithmetic *before*
its result is read: `1 − 0.95⁴ = 18.6 %` for the four sign-arm cell intervals, and
`1 − (1 − 0.0455)⁶ = 24.2 %` for the six consecutive-step comparisons of C5. Neither is used as a
binary gate. The only quantity that gates is a falsified qualitative claim of L341, and none is.

**The trigger probability of C7 is not quoted, and the reason is stated.** The entropy migration
redraws **both** arms, so the legacy arm shares no seed with the submitted campaign and reproducing
it cell by cell is not the question C7 asks. What C7 measures is the protocol specification §1.10 displacement at a
**common** draw, which is a comparison the two arms make exactly.

**The `Γ = 1` grid point leaves the GARCH family.** At `α = 0.08` the value `Γ = 1` is unattainable
inside the family (`compute_gamma_exact(0.08, 0) = 1.1739`); the delivered script reaches it by
simulating `(0, 0)`. This is the structure `R11-gamma-grid-floor` already registers, and it is
cross-referenced rather than duplicated.

## The attribution arm

`--qmle-options legacy` restores the delivered `minimize` call verbatim — no `tol`, no `ftol`, no
`eps`, no output truncation — and changes nothing else. It runs unconditionally, stamps every output
`_legacy_qmle`, and certifies no v87 value.

What it establishes: **protocol specification §1.10 is not what moved the numerals.** Three of the six tables are
**bit-identical** between the two arms (`R17_fpr_baseline`, `R17_fpr_arms`, `R17_misspecification`),
the sign column of the warm-up table is bit-identical, and every false-alarm rate of the warm-up
table is identical to the last digit. The persistence median at the published cell moves by
`−1.4e-4`. The displacement against the submitted campaign is therefore the 128-bit re-keying and
the definitional change, and the compliance requirement costs a fourth decimal.

## Coherence with R04

R04 measured the estimation cost of the parametric route on the **tail** axis; R17 measures it on
the **warm-up** axis. R04's `Eco_L1` arm needs a threshold `1.03` to `1.21` times its `Oracle_Eco`
counterpart to hold the same level across `Γ ∈ {1, 11.58, 50, 200}`, and its estimated arm is slower
than its oracle at every `ν` of the grid. R17 measures the same direction: the shorter the warm-up,
the further the parametric arm sits from the level it holds asymptotically. **The R04 side is
already contested** — `R04b-efficiency-crossing` and `R04b-oracle-ratio-offset` are registered D3 —
so the comparison is written against R04's *regenerated* values and not against L253's printed
`4.9` / `4.6` / `0.3`.

## Known deviations from the submitted manuscript

Three entries reach `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.

**`R17-campaign-redraw` — Class A, D2.** Preamble §S6 requires the trajectory key to carry role and
index alone; the delivered script keyed on `s*77`, `s*77+99`, `s*42+888` and `s*101+nw`. Migrating
redraws every Monte-Carlo value of the four protocols. Four printed numerals of L341 move —
`0.62 → 0.63`, `9.5 % → 10.5 %`, `3.0 % → 7.0 %`, `3`–`8 % → 10`–`11 %` — and every qualitative
claim holds. Pre-classified before the first run by the `R05/R07/R09/R10/R13/R14`-`campaign-redraw`
precedents.

**`R17-sign-arm-crn-degeneracy` — Class A, no severity.** Under a key carrying no grid coordinate
the monitored sign stream is bit-identical across the leverage axis, so the eight cells of
`protocol_3d` hold four readings. No published value is affected — the envelope L341 quotes is a
min–max over the **warm-up** axis, which is not degenerate — but the evidence behind it is narrower
than eight cells suggest, so the entry takes the heavier examination of §S3 rather than the lighter
one.

**`R17-eco-l1-arm-identity` — Class A, D3.** Table 1 (L117) defines `Eco-L1` as
`ε_t/σ̂_t`, QMLE-standardized, monitored for a **location (1st-order)** change. The cell L341 quotes
its `9.5 %` from is `protocol_3d`, which monitors `(ẑ² − μ)/σ` — the **squared**, second-order
statistic — at the `(0.5, 65.0)` operating point. The delivered script distinguishes the two by name
in its own protocol 3b: `adds_eco_l2` is `strict_cusum(z_eco_hat**2 …, 0.5, 65.0)` and
`adds_eco_l1` is `strict_cusum(z_eco_hat, 0.5, 10.0)`. The numeral is not the L1 arm's: at protocol
3b's 100 seeds the rate lattice is `k/100` and `9.5 %` is **unattainable**, whereas `9.5 % = 19/200`
is exactly protocol 3d's resolution. A method description the pipeline does not produce falsifies a
qualitative claim whatever the numeral does, which is the rule `R16-dating-misdescription` fixes at
D3. **The persistence median is not affected**: the QMLE fit is common to both monitors, so
`α̂+β̂` is arm-agnostic.

Two camera-ready candidates carry clarifications that open **no** register entry:
`R17_v87_warmup_restoration_scope.md` and `R17_v87_warmup_resolution.md`.

## Limitations

**Four of the five delivered tables certify nothing v87 prints.** They are regenerated and shipped
because they are the controls under which protocol 3d is read, not because the manuscript quotes
them. Reading L349's numerals off `R17_misspecification.csv` would misattribute them: R12
owns them and runs a different design.

**The `3`–`8 %` envelope has no sampling distribution of its own.** It is reported with a paired
bootstrap and it gates nothing. Any statement about warm-up independence should be read off the
slope and its interval.

**Protocol 3c fits nothing.** It standardizes with the symmetric population limit
`α_sym = α + γ_lev/2`, so its leakage is structural rather than estimation noise. Whether that
mean-matching construction *is* the QMLE pseudo-true parameter is an open question `AUDIT_R12.md`
already poses, and no measurement of this stream decides it.

**The `ML` arm is not an oracle-parameter arm.** It estimates nothing and reads no true parameter.
Its constancy along the three grids is an algebraic identity of the simulators, not evidence of
robustness, and the audit says so at every cell.
