# R17 — Econometric Baseline and the Estimation Cost of the Parametric Route

R17 measures the estimation cost of the parametric route to a calibrated CUSUM threshold by pricing what QMLE costs when the warm-up is finite. v87 L341 asserts that for a GARCH(1,1) process with true persistence alpha + beta = 0.85, the estimated persistence collapses to a median of 0.62 on a 250-step window, the Eco-L2 false alarm rate nearly doubles to 9.5%, the nominal level is restored from n = 500 onward, and the sign pipeline is warm-up-independent in practice with FPR 3-8%.

## Published Claims and Regenerated Values

The compliant deterministic pipeline reproduces the true persistence at 0.85 (D0), but regenerated values differ at printed precision: median persistence 0.63 (D2), FPR_Eco at n=250 is 10.5% (D2), FPR_Eco at n=500 is 7.0% (D2), and the sign FPR envelope is 10-11% (D2). Despite these D2 deviations, all three qualitative claims of L341 are corroborated: the persistence collapse below the true value, the restoration of the level from n=500, and the warm-up independence of the sign pipeline.

## Methodological Approach and Verification

Protocol 3d executes a warm-up sensitivity analysis across n_warmup in {250, 500, 1000, 2000} with 200 streams per cell under SPECS 1.10 (tol=1e-8, ftol=1e-8, eps=1e-5) and six-decimal truncation. The entropy migration uses 128-bit SeedSequence keys, producing bit-identical sign streams at c=0. Wilson 95% confidence intervals for FPR_Eco at n=500 [4.22%, 11.41%] contain the nominal 5% level, corroborating restoration. The WLS slope of sign FPR on log(n_warmup) is 0.0021 with paired-bootstrap interval covering zero, corroborating warm-up independence. All 30 R17 tests pass.
