# R06 — Empirical Validity Map of the Whitening Property

## Contribution to the Manuscript
R06 maps the empirical boundaries of Proposition prop:whitening, establishing that the binary error stream of a non-anticipative classifier predicting the sign of a return remains i.i.d. Bernoulli(1/2) across a 13-point Gamma grid from 1 to 200, despite the t7 innovation grid violating E[eps_t^4] < ∞ beyond Gamma ≈ 41.6. The pooled binary rejection rate of 4.77% covers the nominal 5% level at cluster-robust precision, confirming whitening holds past the fourth-moment boundary. Panel (B) demonstrates sharp scope limits: non-median thresholds or continuous MSE loss re-inherit autocorrelation with 100% rejection for c ≥ 0.5 and for MSE, exactly as Remark rem:scope requires.

## Methodology
The experiment generates 100 streams per configuration (1300 paired in Part A, 500 in Part B) with nu = 7 Student-t innovations, N = 8000 steps, and Ljung-Box test at lag 20 with alpha = 0.05. The GARCH(1,1) generator and task evaluators are copied verbatim from the submitted campaign and asserted byte-identical. A paired design is declared: one seed carries the same label stream to every Gamma, with design effect 3.21 measured and accounted for via cluster-robust intervals. A counterfactual arm with independent per-cell seeds verifies that removing pairing still covers the nominal level.

## Key Findings
All tabular data reproduce the submitted campaign byte-for-byte. The fourth-moment boundary is computed from (alpha, nu) as 41.584288, rounding to 41.6 at manuscript precision (D1 deviation). All pooled and per-cell rejection rates are bit-identical (D0). The median-task control has limited resolution at N = 100, with Wilson interval [3.4%, 13.7%] containing the nominal level but excluding very little. Task boundary saturated cells reach 100% rejection as claimed, and the intermediate threshold c = 0.25 at 44% rejection provides the only measurement of the transition between white and saturated regimes.
