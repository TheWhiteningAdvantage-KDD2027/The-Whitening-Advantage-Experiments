# R02c — Horizon Sweep and Mechanism Test

R02c evaluates two competing explanations for the Ljung-Box over-rejection observed in R02b: **H1** (convergence-rate artifact vanishing with n, driven by the third absolute moment of the autocovariance summand) and **H2** (persistent asymptotic quantile breakdown from the absence of E[eps^8]).

## Methodology

A 3 x 4 factorial design sweeps nu ∈ {5, 6, 7} and n_steps ∈ {2000, 8000, 32000, 128000} with 1000 streams per cell. The Ljung-Box test at lag 20 is applied to both squared and raw innovations. Deterministic seeding uses 128-bit hashes with SeedSequence. A continuity check reproduces the R02b nu=5, n=8000 cell (k_sq=88, k_raw=57) to guarantee cross-stream determinism.

## Results

The horizon slope does not separate H1 from H2: the measured WLS slopes are indistinguishable from zero at 95% confidence for all nu, with the decay-to-nominal slope lying inside the confidence intervals. The witness arm at nu=7 maintains calibration (pooled rejection 5.60% [4.93, 6.36%]), while nu=5 and nu=6 exhibit persistent over-rejection (7.75% [6.96, 8.62%] and 7.72% [6.94, 8.59%]). At nu=5 and n=128000, the rejection rate remains 7.7% [6.2, 9.5%], confirming persistence across a 64-fold horizon increase.

## Impact on the manuscript

R02c constrains the admissible explanation without altering any numerical claim. It rules out attributing the effect to a missing eighth moment (the account nearest to v87 wording), as E[eps^8] is infinite at nu=7 yet no over-rejection is observed. The true mechanism remains untested; the coincidence with the Berry-Esseen boundary at nu > 6 is suggestive but not causal.
