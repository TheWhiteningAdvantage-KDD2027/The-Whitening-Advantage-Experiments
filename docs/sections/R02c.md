## R02c — Horizon Sweep and Eighth-Moment Account Falsification

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target
R02c conducts a horizon-scaling analysis of Ljung-Box test over-rejection rates across stream lengths 2000, 8000, 32000, and 128000 for Student t innovations with nu = 5, 6, 7, establishing that the eighth-moment explanation (E[eps^8] = infinity for nu <= 8) does not survive its own witness. The experiment tests 12 cells (3 nu × 4 horizons × 1000 streams) with lag-20 Ljung-Box on both squared and raw streams, computing Wilson 95% confidence intervals and WLS slopes vs log(horizon). The mathematical target is falsifying the hypothesis that infinite eighth moment universally causes over-rejection.

### Execution & Verification
The pipeline enforces strict S7 determinism with single-threaded BLAS and PYTHONHASHSEED=42. All 7 test suites pass: seed uniqueness (12000 distinct), negative control calibration (pooled raw Wilson CI [4.52%, 5.82%] covers 5%), eighth-moment account refutation (nu=7 Wilson CI [4.93%, 6.36%] covers 5% while nu=5 [6.96%, 8.62%] and nu=6 [6.94%, 8.59%] exclude it), slope test power declaration (all CIs contain zero), control arm integrity (nu=7 contains nominal), continuity with R02b (nu=5, n=8000: 8.8% squared, 5.7% raw), and mechanism slope logic (valid CIs). D2 deviation documented in DEVIATIONS.md.
