# R14 — Efficiency reversal on real Bitcoin

R14 races the recentred sign-CUSUM (`Concept`) against a CUSUM on the honestly standardized
GARCH residual `ε_t/σ̂_t` (`Eco-L1`, refitted on each pre-onset window and filtered **forward
through the observed stream**, no look-ahead) on daily Bitcoin and Ethereum returns, both arms
calibrated to one realized false-alarm rate measured on real placebo windows. It regenerates
`articleB_whitening_v87.tex` Figure 16 (`fig:crypto_race`) and every numeral of **L345**.

**Every published Bitcoin quantity reproduces, bit for bit.** The iso-FPR `4.7%` (`5/106`), the
`106` monthly onsets, `ν̂ = 2.78`, the delay ratio `0.74` at `c = 0.35`, parity `1.01` at
`c = 1.5` and the mean `0.87` are **numerically identical** to the submitted campaign: the whole
`Real_BTC` block of the race table matches the witness on all 13 shared columns with a worst
difference of exactly `0`. The `±1e-6` placebo dither only breaks CUSUM ties, and the realized
false-alarm rate is the discrete count `5/106`, so the entropy migration cannot move the real arm.

**The quasi-Gaussian synthetic control is redrawn and its three numerals move.** v87 prints
`0.98`–`1.14`, mean `1.06`; the re-keyed `t₃₀` control gives **`0.95`–`1.24`, mean `1.04`**. That
is a D2 registered as `R14-campaign-redraw`. **The qualitative claim holds**: the control still
inverts the ordering to `Eco-L1`-faster, and the paired bootstrap interval of the regenerated mean,
`[0.979, 1.069]`, still **covers** the published `1.06`. However, this interval contains `1` (parity): 
the qualitative claim of the inversion rests on a point estimate of `1.04` whose 95% interval spans parity, 
meaning the evidence is weaker than the formulation implies.

**One control fires, on real Ethereum, and it is reported rather than repaired.** The re-keyed
dither moves the `Real_ETH` `Concept` calibration from `3/72` to `4/72` while `Eco` stays at
`3/72`, so that source is no longer iso-FPR and no speed comparison on it is interpretable. v87
publishes no delay and no ordering claim about real Ethereum — L345's own "the fair-coin pivot does
not hold exactly" is the clause this corroborates — so nothing printed is contradicted. See
§"Known deviations" and §"Limitations".

## Execution

```bash
./run_experiment_R14.sh          # measured 23 s total: migrated arm 11.8 s, legacy arm 11.2 s
pytest tests/test_R14_claims.py -v
```

The orchestrator runs the migrated arm and then, **unconditionally**, the `--legacy-seeds`
port-fidelity diagnostic. Running a diagnostic only when a result comes out badly is how a
diagnostic becomes an instrument of selection, so it always runs and its artefacts always ship.

`run_all.sh` discovers `run_experiment_R14.sh` by sorted enumeration; neither `run_all.sh` nor
`run_tests.sh` nor `README.md` is edited by this experiment.

## The measurement

**Four sources, one design.** `Real_BTC` and `Real_ETH` are the daily log-return series;
`Synth_BTC` and `Synth_ETH` are quasi-Gaussian `t₃₀` GARCH(1,1) controls matched on the empirical
variance of their real counterpart and evaluated at **the same onsets**. Onsets are the first
trading day of each month that carries a full `H_ref = 500` past and a full `H_det = 500` future:
`106` on BTC, `72` on ETH.

**Two arms at one operating point.** `Concept` monitors `1{r_t > med(r_ref)} − q̂_ref`; `Eco-L1`
monitors `ε_t/σ̂_t` where `σ̂_t` is the GARCH conditional volatility filtered forward from the
**injected** stream, so the drift inflates the variance exactly as it does for a deployed monitor
and volatility masking is not neutralised. Each arm's threshold is bisected on its own placebo
windows to a realized false-alarm rate near `5%`.

**The drift grid** is `c ∈ {0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.35, 0.5, 0.60, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5}` in
units of the unconditional standard deviation.

**The "reliable range" is derived, not typed.** The delivered `verify_invariants` selects
`c ≥ 0.35` by literal. R14 selects the magnitudes at which **both** arms reach the caption's
`DetRate ≥ 0.9` — the only rule under which a ratio of two `ADD` is computable at all — and on the
submitted campaign the two selections coincide exactly, on seven magnitudes. That agreement,
asserted against the witness by `tests/test_R14_claims.py`, is what licenses replacing the literal.

**C2 fires on `Real_ETH`, and its scope was fixed before the run.** The bisection stops when
`|k/N − 0.05| ≤ 0.005`, and a realized rate can only take the values `k/N`. At `N = 106` exactly
one integer qualifies — `k = 5`, which is v87's `4.7 %` — so the BTC agreement is **forced by the
tolerance**. At `N = 72` **no** integer qualifies: `3/72 = 4.17 %` and `4/72 = 5.56 %` straddle the
band, the bisection exhausts its forty iterations, and any ETH agreement is an outcome of its
dynamics rather than a constraint the calibration enforces. That arithmetic is in the test suite,
independent of the run. The three sources whose *speed* comparison v87 publishes — `Real_BTC`,
`Synth_BTC` and `Synth_ETH` — stop the run if their match fails; a failure anywhere else is stamped
`iso_fpr_matched = False` on every row, read by no macro, and reported.

**C9 and the design effect.** Consecutive onsets are about `21` trading days apart and a detection
window is `H_det = 500` trading days, so two onsets share observations for `⌈500/21⌉ = 24` monthly
steps and are disjoint beyond that. `K = 24` comes from that mechanism and never from the observed
autocorrelation (§S4 rule 8). The delivered `SEM`, `CI_low` and `CI_high` are **kept unchanged**
for witness comparability and `deff`, `n_eff`, `SEM_design`, `CI_low_design` and `CI_high_design`
are added beside them; the figure's band is `SEM_design`. **No printed v87 numeral depends on
either.** On the `Real_BTC` reliable grid the `Eco-L1` arm carries the dependence — `deff` rises
from `1.00` at `c = 0.35` to `6.55` at `c = 1.5`, i.e. `106` detections buying `16.2` independent
readings — while the `Concept` arm stays near `1`. 54 of the 88 cells return a Kish sum below `1`,
which would claim more independent readings than the cell contains; each is clamped to `1.0` with
its own log line.

## Control design under §S4bis

**The delivered direction gate is removed as a gate and kept as a measurement.** `Control (d)` of
the delivered script reads the synthetic ratio at `min(reliable c)` and calls `sys.exit(1)` when it
is at or below `1.05`. A minimum over a grid is an extremum statistic with no sampling
distribution, which the fourth corollary of §S4bis bans outright, and the R14 prompt's own C5 says
*characterise, do not correct*. What replaces it: the ratio at every pairwise-reliable magnitude,
its mean, and a paired moving-block bootstrap envelope. Nothing halts on it.

**Two thresholds, evaluated separately.** A moved numeral and a falsified claim are different
questions and §S3 separates them. L345's qualitative claim about panel B is not the numeral `1.06`
— it is that the `t₃₀` control *inverts the ordering to* `Eco-L1`*-faster*. Its falsification
condition is therefore that the regenerated mean ratio's 95 % interval lies **entirely below 1**.
It does not: `[0.9793, 1.0688]`. The numeral question is separate and its answer is that `1.0410`
rounds to `1.04` and not to `1.06`, which is the D2. A single magnitude below parity falsifies
nothing at all — v87 itself prints a range whose lower end, `0.98`, is already on the other side.

**The extrema ship as extrema.** `\RFourteenRatioSynthMin` and `\RFourteenRatioSynthMax` are a
minimum and a maximum over seven points and have no stable sampling distribution; they are
descriptive, they gate nothing, and the macro file says so in its own header. The two magnitudes
L345 *names* — `c = 0.35` and `c = 1.5` — are read at those grid points and never at an argmin, so
they do carry a stable distribution. The minimum of the regenerated real ratio still sits at
`c = 0.35`.

**The family-wise arithmetic is logged before any result is read.** Over the seven paired
magnitudes of `Real_BTC` a sign test has trigger probability `2 × 0.5⁷ = 1.56 %` under
exchangeability of the two arms; reading a 95 % band as a maximum over seven points would trigger
with probability `1 − 0.95⁷ = 30.2 %` under its own null, which is why the extrema are descriptive.

**The QMLE band is a band on the instrument, and its limit is stated.** The delivered G2 gate is
`median bias < 0.05` and `fallback fraction < 0.10` over 20 recovery simulations. The null
distribution of a median over 20 simulations has no closed form, so no trigger probability is
quoted for it; the gate is kept because it guards the measuring instrument rather than a published
claim, and the log says before reading that a firing would be characterised and reported, never
reconciled by reseeding (§S4.10). It does not fire: the migrated median bias is `0.0228`.

## The legacy-seed diagnostic arm

`--legacy-seeds` restores the delivered `RandomState(100 / 200 / 201 / 300)` draws **and nothing
else**, and stamps `_legacy_seeds` on all four CSVs, the figure, the macro file and the log. It is
a port-fidelity diagnostic: it separates "the re-keying moved panel B" from "the transcription
broke panel B", which is a question about *this port* and not an exploration of how far a published
result holds. **The `_legacy_seeds` artefacts certify no v87 value**, and their macro file says so
in its own header.

What it establishes: against the witness, the diagnostic arm reproduces **all 88 cells of the race
table bit for bit** on `n_onsets`, `FPR_achieved`, `DetRate`, `ADD`, `SEM` and `add_reliable`, and
both diagnostic rows bit for bit on `nu_hat`, `lb_pvalue`, `FPR_C_real` and `FPR_E_real`. Exactly
two quantities move, and both have a mechanism:

| quantity                           | drift     | mechanism                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `lambda_star`, `Real_ETH` / `Eco`  | `7.7e-07` | the only two calibrations that never break early, so `λ` is the endpoint of forty full bisection halvings       |
| `lambda_star`, `Synth_ETH` / `Eco` | `3.4e-07` | the same                                                                                                        |
| `Var_z_hat` (both assets)          | `4.7e-09` | `float_precision='round_trip'` moves the parsed returns by about one ULP; SLSQP's finite tolerance amplifies it |

Neither perturbation changes a single delay: `ADD` moves on `0` of `88` cells. Every movement in
the default arm is therefore attributable to the entropy migration, and the counterfactual that
establishes it has been run rather than argued (§S4.5).

`RandomState(100 / 200 / 201 / 300)` draws to a 128-bit `SeedSequence` keyed on role and index
alone. Three numerals of the quasi-Gaussian control move at v87's printing precision:

| --------- | ------- | ----------- |
| lower end | `0.98`  | **`0.95`**  |

| mean      | `1.06`  | **`1.04`**  |

- **The `Real_ETH` iso-FPR match is lost** (`Concept 4/72` against `Eco 3/72`). v87 makes no delay

- **The unreliable-cell count moves from `25` to `28` of `88`.** v87 prints the rule, not the

- **The QMLE median bias moves from `0.0336` to `0.0228`.** v87 prints neither.

## Limitations

- **`ADD` is conditional on detection at every cell, including the reliable ones.** `DetRate ≥ 0.9`
  is not `1`, so a delay average at a reliable magnitude still conditions on between `90 %` and
  `100 %` of onsets. This is the same conditioning `R09-add-conditioning` registers for Figure 9B,
  and the per-cell `DetRate`, `n_detected` and the full per-onset table ship so that a reader can
  price it.
- **The real Ethereum race is not iso-FPR under the migrated draw**, so its delay ratios compare
  two detectors at two operating points and are not interpretable as a speed comparison. They are
  persisted and printed as description only.
- **The design effect is measured on the delay series and applied to the detection-rate interval.**
  `CI_low_design` and `CI_high_design` evaluate the carried Wilson interval at `n_eff = n_det/deff`,
  where `deff` is the Kish factor of the *delay* mean. That is one plan-level inflation applied to
  two statistics, not two separately measured design effects; no published numeral depends on it.
- **`nu_hat` violates the fourth-moment condition, and that is the regime the figure illustrates.**
  For a standardized Student-`t`, `E|z|^p < ∞ ⟺ p < ν`, so the variance exists iff `ν > 2` and the
  fourth moment iff `ν > 4`. At `ν̂ = 2.78` the variance exists and **the fourth moment does not**,
  which is why the `Γ` penalty and the `χ²` limit of a Ljung–Box on *squared* residuals are
  unjustified on the `Data` pipeline v87 contrasts against. The Ljung–Box actually computed here is
  on the **sign** stream, which is bounded, so its own `χ²` approximation is untouched by this.
- **The synthetic controls are a control, not a second dataset.** They are `t₃₀` GARCH(1,1) paths
  matched on one moment of their real counterpart and read at the same onsets; they establish what
  the same machinery does under light tails and nothing about crypto.
