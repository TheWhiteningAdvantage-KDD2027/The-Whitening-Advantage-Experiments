# R09 — Anytime-valid detection on the fair-coin stream

R09 measures v87 Figure 9 (`fig:anytime`) and the paragraph at `sec:exactness` L243: what happens to a fixed-horizon sign-CUSUM when the monitoring does not stop at the horizon it was calibrated for, and what a mixture of betting martingales delivers instead. Three arms — a sign-CUSUM, a `16 × 3 × 2` mixture martingale (MIX) and an e-process CUSUM (e-CUSUM) — run on the **same** 20 000 fair-coin streams over `[1, 4H]` at seven nominal levels, against a 10-point drift grid on 2 000 drift streams per cell.

**Every qualitative claim of L243 and of the Figure 9 caption holds.** The fixed-horizon CUSUM's
realised false-alarm rate climbs by about a factor of four under continued watching; the mixture stays at or below `α` at all seven levels; the mixture is the faster arm at `η = 0.10`; e-CUSUM clears `ARL₀ ≥ 1/α` with a minimum margin of `20.54×`; and the CUSUM peeking rate exceeds the mixture's at every level, at `22.9`–`68.5` paired standard errors on the same streams. **Three printed numerals move** under the 128-bit re-keying preamble §S6 requires — `18\% → 20\%`, `409 → 410`, `539 → 533` — which is a D2 and the subject of §"Known deviations".

**Two things this stream makes legible that the submitted artefacts do not.** Panel C's CUSUM and
MIX curves are **right-censored at the simulation horizon on 65–99% of streams** and are therefore lower bounds pinned to `4H` rather than average run lengths; and panel B's delay is a **conditional** mean over the streams that alarmed inside `(τ, H]`, at detection rates running from `5.70%` to `97.60%`. Neither falsifies anything v87 prints — the panel C caption names e-CUSUM alone, whose censoring is at most `0.055%` — and both are registered, measured and drawn.

`run_experiment_R09.sh` passes `--control-arms ecusum` **explicitly**, so the certified run produces the audit-only e-CUSUM control arm. `NUM_CHUNKS` is fixed at `10`, so the chunk decomposition — and therefore every output — is independent of `--n-jobs`. The script is a single stage: the figure and the seven macros are computed from the in-memory frames, and those frames are then re-serialised and their digests compared with the files just written, so the figure is certified to describe the persisted campaign rather than assumed to.

`run_all.sh` discovers `run_experiment_R09.sh` by sorted enumeration; neither `run_all.sh` nor `run_tests.sh` is edited by this experiment.

## The measurement

**Constants, carried verbatim from the witness** and traced to their defining lines: `H = 5000`,
`τ = 2500`, `T_ext = 4H = 20 000`, `δ_CUSUM = 0.1`, seven levels `α ∈ {0.10, 0.07, 0.05, 0.035,
0.025, 0.015, 0.01}`, ten drifts `η ∈ {0.02 … 0.20}`, `N_CAL = 50 000`, `N_NULL = 20 000`,
`N_ALT = 2 000`, `NUM_CHUNKS = 10`. The mixture is `96 = 16` start times `× 3` betting fractions `× 2` sides at uniform weight `1/96`.

**Three arms and three different guarantees.**

| arm      | alarm rule                   | guarantee             | threshold             |
| -------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| `CUSUM`  | `max(S⁺, S⁻) ≥ λ*`           | fixed horizon `[1,H]` | calibrated on `N_CAL` |
| `MIX`    | `logsumexp(logM) ≥ −log α`   | Ville, time-uniform   | `1/α`, exact          |
| `eCUSUM` | `max(logM⁺, logM⁻) ≥ −log α` | `ARL₀` only           | `1/α`, exact          |

**Three stopping protocols**, and the third is the one v87 does not describe: `nominal` is
`{fa ≤ H}`, `peeking` is `{fa ≤ 4H}`, and `extended` is the **crossing state at exactly `t = 4H`**, not a cumulative probability. Panel A plots all three. `peeking` dominates the other two **by construction** — `{fa ≤ H}` and `{cross at 4H}` are both subsets of `{fa ≤ 4H}` — which is why the two `…PeekingFprMax` macros are always the peeking bar.

**Everything is paired.** Every entropy key carries a role and an index and never a process
parameter, so the same key serves every grid point: the three arms read the same `y_t` inside a chunk, and the H1 campaign draws one uniform block per chunk **before** the `η` loop, so two drifts consume the identical uniform stream and differ only where the threshold moves. Every between-arm comparison at fixed `(α, η)`, and every comparison across `η` and across `α`, is paired by construction. No pooled interval is published; the one paired comparison control C4 reads ships with its measured Kish design effect.

**Stream-level family-wise trigger probability, logged once before any result is read:**
`1 − (1 − 0.01)⁴ = 3.9404%` for the four gated statistics, `4.0365%` including the calibration coherence and M1(i) gates — below the 5% ceiling §S4bis fixes. No level was chosen after a result was seen.

**C3 is designed against the instrument the obvious approach would use.** Seven binary gates at 5%
would ring on a compliant campaign with probability `1 − 0.95⁷ = 30.2%`; worse, the mixture's peeking rate sits at `0.94`–`1.00` of `α`, so at `α = 0.05` the margin to the bound is `0.00055` against a binomial SE of `0.00154` — `0.36σ`, roughly one chance in three of a spurious exceedance at that level alone. A two-sided KS test of the seven p-values is also wrong: under Ville they are
*stochastically large* by construction, so a uniformity test would reject **because** the bound is
conservative. The claim is one-sided domination, so the statistic is one-sided: `F_U(α) ≤ α` over the whole level range, tested with `D⁺ = sup(F_n(α) − α)` against the least-favourable boundary. `U` has an atom at `1` of mass `0.0046` — the streams whose mixture never exceeds `1` — which sits above every `α` and cannot inflate `D⁺`. M1(ii) supplies an independent replicate on a disjoint key (`D⁺ = 7.54e-4`, `p = 0.977`); the two are reported side by side and neither is averaged into the other.

**C3 carries its own negative control.** The same statistic on the CUSUM arm, with `U` read off the
`N_CAL = 50 000` calibration survival function, gives `D⁺ = 0.4775` and a nominal `p = 0`. Two caveats travel with it and neither is repaired: that `U` uses an **estimated** CDF, which carries the double variance of §S4bis's second corollary, and that the CUSUM statistic lives on a lattice, so `U` has heavy ties under which the `ksone` tail is not exact. The effect size dwarfs both. This direction is a control on **power**, not an acceptance gate.

**C4 replaces nine adjacent comparisons per arm with one aggregate.** Nine pairwise gates at `p`
would ring with probability `1 − (1 − p)⁹ = 8.65%` at `p = 0.01`, and strict adjacent monotonicity is unusable anyway: in the submitted campaign the CUSUM pair `η = 0.02 → 0.04` moves `1244.36 → 1234.90`, `−9.5` steps against a difference SE of order `77`. The Spearman with its exact `10!`-permutation null resolves the same question in one statistic, and it does so on all three arms. The nine adjacent z-margins are logged descriptively and gate nothing.

## The third arm, and why it is outside the published path

v87 draws panel B with **two** curves and its caption names CUSUM and MIX; e-CUSUM appears in panel C's caption alone, and the delivered `simulate_h1` returns keys for those two arms only. R09 keeps the published path at two curves and computes e-CUSUM's H1 delay under an explicit `--control-arms ecusum`, landing in `results/R09_eprocess_anytime/data/R09_eprocess_race_control_ecusum.csv`, whose **filename and `arm` column both stamp the branch** (preamble §S4.3). **That file reproduces nothing v87 prints.** It enters neither `docs/DEVIATIONS.md` nor any camera-ready candidate.

It is computed because a monotone delay response to `η` is the positive control that separates a slow detector from a blind one, and the answer is neither: e-CUSUM's conditional delay falls monotonically from `145.7` to `45.0` steps across the grid (`ρ = −1`, exact `p = 2.76e-7`), so the arm is not blind — but its detection rate inside `(τ, H]` is `0.003` at **every** drift, because at `α = 0.05` its H₀ `ARL₀` is `480` steps against a change point at `τ = 2500`: on `99.7%` of streams the first alarm does not land in `(τ, H]` at all, having already occurred before the change, and only the `6` survivors per cell are counted. The rate is *identical* across `η` because the pre-change data are shared by common random numbers, so the set of streams surviving to `τ` does not depend on the drift. The e-CUSUM arm of panel A is `1.0000` at six of seven levels for the same reason: it is an `ARL₀` device, not a level-`α` test, and that is what makes the caption's "only MIX" true.

The arm consumes **no additional randomness** — it recurses on the same per-chunk `y_t` — so a run without the flag produces byte-identical published CSVs. That identity is the third reproducibility axis of C6.

## Known deviations from the submitted manuscript

Three entries are registered in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`. One is a D2; two carry no severity.

**`R09-campaign-redraw` — L243 and Figure 9A, Class A, D2.** The 128-bit re-keying and the change of
draw mechanism from `rng.binomial(1, p, size)` to `(rng.random(size) < p)` redraw the campaign by construction. The two delays move by `+0.24` and `−0.44` standard errors of their own difference — an ordinary redraw. The peeking rate does not: `0.1801 → 0.1988` is `+4.77` SE. The channel is documented and traceable: `max_M` lives on a `0.2` lattice. The calibration threshold is estimated by an empirical quantile on a finite sample (`N_CAL = 50 000`), so it lands on one or the other attainable point. `np.quantile(max_M, 0.95)` returned `λ* = 11.4` on the submitted calibration sample against `11.2` on the regenerated one. As certified by R07 and read at `float_precision='round_trip'` from `results/R07_estimated_mean/data/R07_lattice_exact_law.csv`, these are adjacent lattice points bracketing the nominal level, with exact attainable levels of `4.29%` at `λ = 11.4` and `5.03%` at `λ = 11.2`. Both are legitimate outputs of the same estimator. The two thresholds achieve the same level at the horizon they were calibrated for (`0.0504` against `0.05034`); peeking to `4H` amplifies the one-step difference. Four of seven levels moved one step, three did not.
**R09 does not separate the threshold channel from the fresh-draw channel** — §S4.5 forbids asserting 
a decomposition the measurement does not establish — and the separation is carried as an open question in `AUDIT_R09.md`. Candidate: `docs/camera_ready_candidates/R09_v87_anytime_numerals.md`.

**`R09-arl0-censoring` — Figure 9 caption (C), Class A, no severity.** CUSUM's run lengths are
right-censored on `65.10%`–`95.54%` of streams and MIX's on `90.52%`–`99.06%`, so both means converge mechanically on `T_ext = 20 000`: `mean(min(fa, T_ext))` is bounded below by `censored_frac · T_ext` by arithmetic, and the MIX point at `α = 0.01` reads `19 842` against a floor of `19 811`. **Nothing printed is falsified**, because the caption's `ARL₀` claim names e-CUSUM alone, whose censoring is at most `0.055%`. Candidate: `docs/camera_ready_candidates/R09_v87_arl0_censoring.md`.

**`R09-add-conditioning` — L243 and Figure 9 caption (B), Class A, no severity.** Panel B's delay is
conditional on an alarm in `(τ, H]`, and the two arms condition on different events: at `η = 0.02` the mixture detects `16.15%` of streams against the CUSUM's `5.70%`, so its conditional mean averages over the hard streams the CUSUM never reaches and comes out `+212` steps higher (`z = +2.89`). The severity was fixed by a decision rule written before the first number was read. The primary instrument is a **matched-detection-rate quantile** — at each `η`, set `q = min(p_CUSUM, p_MIX)` and compare the `q`-quantile of each arm's alarm-time distribution with non-detections at `+∞` — which conditions on nothing. It places the mixture **`1 112` steps faster at `η = 0.02`** (paired bootstrap `[−1242, −964]`) and `1 504` faster at `η = 0.04` (`[−1562, −1427]`), so the marginal reversal is an artefact of conditioning and the entry carries no severity. Candidate: `docs/camera_ready_candidates/R09_v87_delay_parity_scope.md`.

## What is reported and not registered

**Three delivered gates are removed as gates and become classification.** `Priorite_22:619`
(`|FPR − 0.1801| > 1e-9`) and `:631` (`|ADD − 409.1131405377981| > 1e-9`, `|ADD − 538.8051546391753| > 1e-9`) are equality tests at machine precision on Monte-Carlo values the script itself produced. §S7 forbids anchoring an assertion on such a value, and the re-keying redraws the campaign by construction, so all three would fail on the first run and their only exit would be a widened tolerance. They are replaced by the D0–D3 classification against the vendored witness CSVs, read with `float_precision='round_trip'`. The delivered `if fpr > a + 0.005` of M1(ii) goes the same way — `0.005` is derived from nothing — and control C3 replaces it. The delivered `(c)` floor of `0.80` on the e-CUSUM peeking rate is likewise reported with its Wilson interval and gates nothing.

**The delivered controls `(b)` and `(e)` are replaced rather than kept.** Both compare the held-out
level against the calibration level using `sqrt(α(1−α)/N_NULL)`, which ignores that `λ*` is itself estimated on `N_CAL = 50 000` streams. The correct variance of the difference is `α(1−α)(1/N_NULL + 1/N_CAL)`, an SE of `0.001823` against the delivered `0.001541` — understated by a factor `1.18`. The measured gap is `1.706` SE.

**`--fast` is dropped.** It is a second, unstamped parameter set reachable by a flag, and the
repository certifies one configuration.

## Limitations

- **`ADD` is conditional in the submitted campaign too.** `add_c` and `add_m` are means over
`(fa > τ) & (fa ≤ H)` in the delivered `simulate_h1`, at a detection rate of `0.9855` for the `409` figure — a `1.5%` censoring rate at the published operating point. That is what v87 measures; R09 records it and does not change it.
- **The `18\%` displacement is not decomposed.** The lattice step in `λ*` and the fresh `H₀` draw
both contribute and this campaign does not separate them.
- **The mechanism of the mixture's conservatism is not established.** `FPR/α` sits between `0.945`
and `1.000`; whether that is the discrete `16 × 3 × 2` support, the finite peeking horizon, or both, is not separated here, and §S4.5 forbids asserting a mechanism the measurement does not establish.
- **The "extended" protocol is not described by v87.** It is the crossing state at exactly
`t = 4H`, not a cumulative probability, and panel A plots it. Whether a single-look-at-`4H` protocol belongs in the published panel is a question `AUDIT_R09.md` poses and does not settle.
- **The common-detection subset disagrees with the primary instrument at the smallest drift.** Both
readings ship; the subset is selected by both detectors at once and gates nothing.
- **`N_ALT = 2 000` fixes panel B's resolution.** Its SEMs are `3.16×` wider than panels A and C's
for the same underlying dispersion, and the axis says so.
