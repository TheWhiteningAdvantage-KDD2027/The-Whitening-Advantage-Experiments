# R02 — Ljung-Box Whiteness Verification

R02 provides the empirical backbone of Proposition 1: 360 independent stationary GARCH(1,1) streams verify that the binary error stream of a non-anticipative online Hoeffding Tree classifier on a sign-prediction task is i.i.d. Bernoulli(1/2) under conditionally symmetric innovations, regardless of volatility clustering. The experiment covers three regimes (IID, Cal. A with Γ ∈ [4, 8], Cal. B with Γ ∈ [32, 110]), four ETF calibrations, and 30 seeds.

## Methodology

Each stream (n = 8000, lag-20 Ljung-Box) uses 128-bit deterministic seeding and generates squared returns εₜ² and binary errors eₜᵇⁱⁿ. The Data pipeline monitors εₜ² and rejects whiteness in 100% of clustered calibrations (p < 10⁻¹⁰), while the Concept pipeline on eₜᵇⁱⁿ maintains the nominal level: 5.00% (IID), 3.33% (Cal. A), 4.17% (Cal. B), with pooled rate 4.17% and Wilson 95% CI [2.54%, 6.76%].

## Deviation Summary

Two numerical shifts occur against v87. The pooled binary-error rate moves from 4.4% to 4.17% (D2), and the Wilson interval from [2.8%, 7.1%] to [2.54%, 6.76%] (D2); the nominal 5% level remains covered. The IID arm rejection changes from 9.2% to 5.83% (D3 for R02b), indicating the original over-rejection claim is not reproduced with corrected 128-bit seeding. Cross-stream independence is verified via 18 Bonferroni-corrected Pearson tests, all passing at α = 0.05/6.
