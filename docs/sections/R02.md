# R02 — Ljung-Box Whiteness Verification

R02 provides the empirical backbone of Proposition 1: 360 independent stationary GARCH(1,1) streams verify that the binary error stream of a non-anticipative online Hoeffding Tree classifier on a sign-prediction task is i.i.d. Bernoulli(1/2) under conditionally symmetric innovations, regardless of volatility clustering. The experiment covers three regimes (IID, Cal. A with Γ ∈ [4, 8], Cal. B with Γ ∈ [32, 110]), four ETF calibrations (SPY, PFF, VNQ, BWX), and 30 seeds per configuration.

## Methodology

Each stream (n = 8000, lag-20 Ljung-Box) uses 128-bit deterministic seeding via MD5 hash injection. The Data pipeline tests whiteness on squared returns εₜ², while the Concept pipeline applies the same test to binary classification errors eₜᵇⁱⁿ from a Hoeffding Tree predicting the sign of εₜ. Strict single-threaded execution is enforced through environment pinning and harness-level confinement.

## Results

The Data pipeline rejects whiteness in 100% of clustered calibrations (p < 10⁻¹⁰), confirming strong autocorrelation in εₜ². The Concept pipeline maintains the nominal level with per-regime rejection rates of 5.00% (IID), 3.33% (Cal. A), and 4.17% (Cal. B). The pooled binary-error rejection rate is 4.2% with Wilson 95% CI [2.5%, 6.8%], covering the nominal 5% level. The IID arm data rejection rate is 5.8%.

## Deviation Summary

Two numerical shifts occur against v87. The pooled binary-error rate moves from 4.4% to 4.2% (D2), and the Wilson interval from [2.8%, 7.1%] to [2.5%, 6.8%] (D2); the nominal 5% level remains covered. The IID arm rejection changes from 9.2% to 5.8% (D3 for R02b), indicating the original over-rejection claim is not reproduced with corrected 128-bit seeding. Cross-stream independence is verified via 18 Bonferroni-corrected Pearson tests, all passing at α = 0.05/6.
