## R02 — Ljung-Box Whiteness on Multi-ETF GARCH Streams

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target
R02 corroborates the Whitening Proposition on 360 independent stationary GARCH(1,1) streams (30 seeds × 4 ETF calibrations × 3 clustering regimes, n=8000) with standardized t7 innovations. The target is to corroborate that binary classification errors from a sign-prediction task form a martingale difference sequence under H0, while squared innovations exhibit autocorrelation due to volatility clustering. Three regimes are tested: IID (Γ=1), Calibration A (Γ∈[3.90,8.32]), and Calibration B (Γ∈[31.94,110.49]). Ljung-Box Q-tests at lag 20 quantify serial dependence on both ε_t^2 and e_t^bin streams.

### Execution & Verification
The pipeline enforces strict S7 determinism with single-threaded BLAS and PYTHONHASHSEED=42. All 8 test suites pass: stream counts (360), classifier integrity (River v0.23.0), data rejection rates (100% for Cal. A and B), distinct p_concept values (120 per regime), independence diagnostics (18 Pearson tests pass Bonferroni correction), IID arm rejection rate (5.8% within 0-25% bounds), Wilson CI coverage ([2.5%, 6.8%] covers α=0.05), and max clustered p-value (5.26e-18 < 1e-10). D2 deviation documented in DEVIATIONS.md: IID data rejection shifts from 9.2% to 5.8% due to BLAS threading effects on path generation, but the over-rejection qualitative claim remains valid.
