## R04b — Nu Grid Refinement and Efficiency Crossing Point Resolution

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target

R04b resolves the efficiency crossing point that R04 bracketed but could not pinpoint. It re-measures three arms (Eco-L1, Oracle-Eco, Concept) at Gamma = 11.58, c = 0.5, with 2000 null streams of length 5000, on a refined twelve-point nu grid {4.0, 4.5, 5.0, 6.0, 7.0, 8.0, 9.0, 10.0, 12.0, 15.0, 20.0, 30.0} spanning the (7, 30) void where the delay ratio ADD_Concept / ADD_Eco-L1 crosses unity. The mathematical target is Appendix Figure A3 and the crossing estimators: grid bracket (model-free, resolution-limited), inferential bracket (model-free with 95% confidence), shape fit with stream-level bootstrap, and analytic root of 1/(4 f_z(0)^2).

### Execution & Verification

The pipeline enforces strict S7 determinism with single-threaded BLAS, MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE, and PYTHONHASHSEED=42. All 21 test suites pass, confirming cardinalities, bisection convergence to 5% FPR, calibration diagnostics, continuity with R04 at five common nu points, and crossing estimator consistency. The pooled held-out level meets the promised band [4.7%, 5.3%]. Continuity omnibus tests yield p = 0.4498 (Eco-L1) and p = 0.5736 (Oracle_Eco), both exceeding the 0.01 gate. Bootstrap variance inflation factor sqrt(2) = 1.413 corroborates the design effect. Shape fits pass goodness tests (Eco-L1: weighted R^2 = 0.9904, p = 0.5929; Oracle: weighted R^2 = 0.9855, p = 0.2960).
