# R04b — Resolution of the Efficiency Crossing Point (Appendix Figure A3)

R04 measured the delay ratio ADD_Concept / ADD_Eco-L1 against Student-t degrees of freedom on the grid {3, 4, 4.5, 5, 7, 30} and found it crossing unity inside the unsampled interval (7, 30). R04b re-measures the same three arms at Gamma = 11.58, c = 0.5, and 2000 streams on twelve nu values {4, 4.5, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 20, 30}, resolving the crossing.

## Methodology

All sixteen scientific primitives are copied verbatim from R04 and asserted byte-identical at start-up. Four crossing estimators are reported for each arm: grid bracket, inferential bracket, shape fit with bootstrap interval, and analytic root. The bootstrap resamples both calibration and drifted streams, pricing the calibration error of each threshold. Continuity with R04 is verified at five common nu points.

## Results

The Eco-L1 crossing is enclosed by [7, 9] with 95% confidence and estimated at 8.10 [7.78, 8.37] by the primary shape-fit estimator. The oracle crossing is enclosed by [4, 5] and estimated at 4.47 [4.31, 4.57]. The estimation cost is 3.62 [3.31, 3.92] degrees of freedom by the shape-fit route, or 3.22 [2.52, 3.82] by the model-free route.

## Deviation Summary

v87's nu* ~ 4.9 is falsified (D3): the measured bracket [7, 9] excludes it by a wide margin. The oracle crossing at 4.6 lies inside its measured bracket and survives. The published estimation cost of 0.3 is falsified (D3): all measured intervals exclude it by an order of magnitude. The oracle ratio exhibits a systematic +5% offset above its analytic prediction across all grid points.
