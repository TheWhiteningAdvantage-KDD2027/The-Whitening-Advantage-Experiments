# R07 — Whitening under an estimated conditional mean

R07 measures `articleB_whitening_v87.tex` Figure 7 (`fig:estmean`) and the paragraph at
L302–L308: what survives of the whitening property when the conditional mean is **estimated**
rather than known. Six arms on AR(1)-GARCH(1,1) streams — `NAIVE` (µ̂ₜ = 0), `ORACLE`
(µ̂ₜ = φ rₜ₋₁) and four rolling-OLS windows `n ∈ {125, 250, 500, 1000}` — over
`φ ∈ {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.075, 0.1, 0.125, 0.15}`, 10 000 paired trajectories per φ, horizon
`H = 5 000`.

**The paragraph's qualitative content reproduces, and one printed bound does not.** The naive
boundary is a slope: Ljung–Box rejection climbs monotonically from `4.9%` at φ = 0 to `99.8%` at
φ = 0.15, dragging the `Concept` FPR to `21.0%`, and every rolling-OLS arm sits inside the oracle
band on the same paths. **L308's bound on the estimator bias does not hold**: v87 states that
`E[φ̂] − φ` "stays under `2.9 × 10⁻³`" and the regenerated campaign reaches **`3.13 × 10⁻³`** at
(φ = 0.15, n = 125). That is a **D3** and it is the subject of §"Known deviations" below.

**Two numerals of the Figure 7 caption are not R07's.** The caption's `4.29%` / `5.03%` pair is
printed at L241 and sourced there to `2 × 10⁵` fair-coin streams — the campaign the repository's
stream map assigns to **R08** (Figure 8, panel C). R07 does not re-run it, opens no register entry
on it and files no camera-ready candidate against L241. What R07 computes instead is the **exact**
law of the 2δ lattice, by an absorbing-chain dynamic program that consumes no entropy at all and
that agrees to the last bit with exhaustive enumeration of all 2^H paths at `H ∈ {8, 10, 12}`. Its
values, `4.3428%` and `5.1021%`, are handed to R08 in
`docs/camera_ready_candidates/R07_v87_lattice_handoff_to_R08.md`.

## The measurement

**The design is paired, and that is a design choice with a price.** The entropy plan preamble
§S6 mandates keys the trajectory on its **index alone**, so the same innovation stream serves all
seven φ. In the DGP, `h[t]` and `ε[t]` never reference φ — only `r[t] = φ r[t−1] + ε[t]` does — so
the oracle residual `r_curr − φ r_prev` is exactly `ε_curr` at every φ, and **the `ORACLE` arm is
bit-identical across the seven grid points**. The `ORACLE` curve of Figure 7 is exactly flat, and
that is a property of the design, not a measurement.

| quantity                                                    | value                                   | v87 prints |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Ljung–Box rejection, `NAIVE`, φ = 0                        | `4.92%`                                 | `5.1%`     |
| Ljung–Box rejection, `NAIVE`, φ = 0.15                     | `99.79%`                                | `99.8%`    |
| `Concept` FPR, `NAIVE`, φ = 0.15                           | `21.00%`                                | `20.8%`    |
| Ljung–Box rejection over the 28 OLS cells                   | `4.70–5.63%`                            | `4.6–5.6%` |
| `Concept` FPR over the 28 OLS cells                         | `4.84–5.61%`                            | `4.3–5.9%` |
| largest \|`E[φ̂] − φ`\|                                     | **`3.13 × 10⁻³`** at (0.15, 125)        | `< 2.9 × 10⁻³` |
| η at `n = 125`, largest over φ                              | `11.48%` of σ_unc                       | `11.4%`    |
| `E[(α z² + β)²]`, closed form                               | `1.00517456`                            | `1.005`    |
| exact attainable levels at `λ = 11.4` / `11.2`              | `4.3428%` / `5.1021%`                   | `4.29%` / `5.03%` (R08's) |

**λ\* is fixed by the rule L241 states, not by a sample quantile.** The delivered script took
`np.quantile(Ms_cal, 0.95)` on 20 000 calibration streams. On the exact law that estimator returns
the lattice point `11.4` with probability **`0.7380`**: a re-keyed calibration had roughly one
chance in four of printing a different λ\* for reasons that have nothing to do with the science.
L241 states its own rule — "we take the nearest attainable level at or below nominal" — and the
repository implements it on the exact law. **λ\* = 11.4 does not move**, and it is bit-identical to
the float64 of the literal v87 prints.

**The threshold test delivers the upper attainable level, and v87's footnote about it holds.**
L241's footnote states that "floating-point accumulation leaves `M_H` a few ulps above its exact
lattice value, so the implemented test `M_H > λ*` **is** the mathematical `M_H ≥ λ*`". R07 measured
that statement rather than assuming it, on three independent sets of fair-coin streams — its own
10 000 `ORACLE` sign streams (fair coins under H₀ by the proposition being tested), the 20 000
calibration streams and the 5 000 validation streams.

| stream set          | `M` above its lattice value | below | exactly on | disagreements with `M > λ*` | with `M ≥ λ*` |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- | ----- | ---------- | --------------------------- | ------------- |
| `ORACLE`, φ = 0     | 9 652                       | 137   | 211        | 88                          | **0**         |
| calibration         | 19 283                      | 280   | 437        | 137                         | **0**         |
| validation          | 4 828                       | 63    | 109        | 42                          | **0**         |

**Over all 35 000 streams the implemented test coincides exactly with `M ≥ λ*`** and differs from
`M > λ*` on 267. The realised level is `5.16%` on the `ORACLE` streams and `5.064%` on the 25 000
independent fair-coin ones, against `4.3428%` for the exact `M > λ*` and `5.1021%` for the exact
`M ≥ λ*`. The identity is **empirical, not structural**: the float `M` lands *below* its exact
lattice value on 480 of those 35 000 streams, so a stream whose exact maximum equals λ\* can in
principle be missed, and the statement is made for this threshold, this horizon and this
accumulation order only.

**The estimation noise does not decay in 1/√n.** Fitting `log η = a + b log n` per trajectory over
the four windows and averaging the 10 000 slopes — the trajectory is the only i.i.d. unit of this
design, so this interval needs no design-effect correction — gives

```
b̂(η)         = -0.4378   95% [-0.4401, -0.4355]    53.2 standard errors from -0.5
b̂(sd(φ̂))    = -0.4580   95% [-0.4605, -0.4554]    32.3 standard errors from -0.5
```

The prompt's §2.2 makes the observation decisive: **a decay that is not 1/√n invalidates reading
panel B as a window-size effect.** The observation stands whatever the mechanism is; the mechanism
is **not identified** (see below).

**Every pooled interval carries its design effect.** All 42 cells share the same 10 000 innovation
streams, so no two are independent. Control C4 measures the Kish design effect before any pooled
interval is read:

| block    | statistic     | cells | ρ̄        | deff    | n_eff  |
| -------- | ------------- | ----- | --------- | ------- | ------ |
| `ORACLE` | `fpr_concept` | 7     | **1.0**   | **7.0** | **10 000** |
| `NAIVE`  | `fpr_concept` | 7     | `0.6015`  | `4.61`  | `15 187` |
| `OLS`    | `fpr_concept` | 28    | `0.6579`  | `18.76` | `14 924` |
| all      | `fpr_concept` | 42    | `0.6085`  | `25.95` | `16 187` |

The `ORACLE` row is exact and rests on the bit-identity of its seven columns, not on a float64
correlation estimate. **The reference band against which the Figure 7 caption asserts that "all
rolling-OLS arms match oracle false-alarm control" is carried by 10 000 effective trajectories,
not 70 000, so its half-width is √7 = 2.65 times what an independent design would have bought.**
The figure's `ORACLE` legend key carries that `n_eff`.

**C3's gate was re-derived, as §S4bis's fifth corollary requires.** The prompt prescribes a KS
test of the 42 per-cell p-values against `Uniform(0,1)`. That prescription does not survive
re-derivation on this stream's plan: all 42 cells share all 10 000 trajectories, and the seven
`ORACLE` p-values are the same number repeated, so the KS null's independence assumption fails
outright. The KS statistic is reported (`D = 0.2914`, `p = 0.0012`) and is **not** the acceptance
criterion. The criterion is the instrument the paired design actually buys: a McNemar comparison of
each of the 28 OLS cells against `ORACLE` at the same φ on the same trajectories, the maximum
absolute paired difference read against the null law of that maximum under a trajectory-level
sign-flip. Observed maximum `0.0071` at (φ = 0.02, `OLS-1000`) on 415 discordant trajectories,
against a `99.9%` null quantile of `0.0086`; null exceedance probability `0.0118`. **Criterion
met.**

## Known deviations from the submitted manuscript

### D3 — L308's bound on the estimator bias is exceeded (`R07-bias-bound-exceeded`)

v87 L308 states: "the systematic one, the classical small-sample AR bias `E[φ̂] − φ ≈ −2.5 φ/n`,
stays under `2.9 × 10⁻³`". The regenerated campaign gives a largest `|E[φ̂] − φ|` of
**`3.1269 × 10⁻³`**, at (φ = 0.15, n = 125), with a standard error of `1.575 × 10⁻⁴` over the
10 000 trajectories. The observed maximum is `+1.44` standard errors past the printed bound.

**No parameter, tolerance, seed or bound was moved to reconcile this**, and three things are worth
stating beside it, none of which excuses the violation:

1. **The argmax is structurally determined, not selected.** `−2.5 φ/n` is largest at the largest φ
   and the shortest window, which is exactly the cell the maximum lands in. The extremum therefore
   carries the standard error of its own cell rather than the law of a maximum over 28 cells.
2. **v87's own approximation already exceeds its own bound.** `−2.5 φ/n` evaluated at
   (φ = 0.15, n = 125) is `−3.0 × 10⁻³`, which is larger in magnitude than the `2.9 × 10⁻³`
   printed in the same sentence. The regenerated maximum sits `+0.81` standard errors from that
   prediction — i.e. it agrees with the formula v87 prints and disagrees with the bound v87 prints.
3. **The submitted campaign's own witness sits at `2.8697 × 10⁻³`**, i.e. `0.19` standard errors
   *below* the bound and `1.15` standard errors of the difference below the regenerated value. The
   bound reads as a literal transcribed from one Monte-Carlo realisation: the delivered script's
   certification block gated on `max_bias < 2.9e-3`, a threshold its own output had just produced.

**Impact on the manuscript.** The sentence's qualitative claim — that the systematic channel is
small and that bias, not dispersion, is what the property is exposed to — is untouched: `3.1e-3` is
still three orders of magnitude below the momentum coefficients being estimated. What does not
survive is the *numeral read as a bound*. The camera-ready candidate
`R07_v87_bias_bound.md` proposes replacing the bound by the quantity that does hold.

### D2 — the campaign is redrawn (`R07-campaign-redraw`)

The 128-bit re-keying on role and index moves every Monte-Carlo value of the submitted campaign;
40 of the 42 Ljung–Box cells and all 42 FPR cells changed. Four printed numerals move at v87's
printing precision: `5.1% → 4.9%`, `20.8% → 21.0%`, the Ljung–Box envelope `4.6–5.6% → 4.7–5.6%`
and η at `n = 125` `11.4% → 11.5%`. Every qualitative claim of L308 that these support holds.
Pre-classified Class A / D2 by the `R11-regenerated`, `R05-campaign-redraw` and
`R13-campaign-redraw` precedents.

### The `ORACLE` reference band is carried by 10 000 trajectories, not 70 000 (`R07-oracle-band-precision`)

The same re-keying collapses seven independent `ORACLE` measurements into one repeated seven
times. This **bounds the empirical corroboration of the Figure 7 caption; it touches no theorem
and no printed value** (the R18 precedent). Measured in `R07_design_effect.csv`; the figure's
`ORACLE` legend key states the `n_eff`.

### λ\* came from an estimator astride a lattice boundary (`R07-lambda-star-estimator`)

Reported, no printed value moves: λ\* = 11.4 either way. See "The measurement" above.

### Figure 7 panel B operates at the upper attainable level (`R07-panelB-operating-level`)

The `ORACLE` mean FPR is `5.16%`, `+0.26` standard errors from the level the operator delivers
(`5.10%`) and `+3.69` from the "exact lattice level `4.29%`" the caption's parenthetical attaches
to λ\*. Both of the caption's statements are individually true — λ\* *does* carry that exact level
under the mathematical `>` — and together they invite the reader to attribute `4.29%` to the panel.
No printed value moves; this is a clarification, and the camera-ready candidate
`R07_v87_panelB_operating_level.md` proposes the wording.

## Reported and not registered

**The η exponent gets no register entry**: v87 prints no exponent, so no printed value moves. The
observation is nonetheless the one the prompt's §2.2 makes decisive, and it is stated above.

**The mechanism behind it is not identified.** Control C8 runs a three-rung ladder, audit-only,
2 000 trajectories per rung at φ ∈ {0, 0.15}, with the reading rule fixed *before* the first
number: b̂ → −0.5 on `gauss_garch` supports the fourth-moment reading, b̂ → −0.5 only on
`gauss_iid` supports the persistence reading, neither or both and the cause is reported as not
identified.

| `dgp_arm`     | innovations     | (α, β)             | `E[(αz²+β)²]` | persistence | b̂(η) at φ = 0.15 |
| ------------- | --------------- | ------------------ | ------------- | ----------- | ------------------ |
| `t7_garch`    | standardized t₇ | (0.1058, 0.8742)   | `1.005175`    | `0.98`      | `-0.4420 ± 0.0027` |
| `gauss_garch` | Gaussian        | (0.1058, 0.8742)   | `0.982787`    | `0.98`      | `-0.4601 ± 0.0025` |
| `gauss_iid`   | Gaussian        | (0, 0)             | `0`           | `0`         | `-0.5188 ± 0.0024` |

Neither rung returns −0.5: `gauss_garch` moves the exponent only a third of the way and
`gauss_iid` overshoots past it. **The cause is not identified**, and no text of this stream says
that the fourth moment causes the departure. Removing the fourth-moment excess *and* the volatility
clustering together changes the exponent by `0.077`, of which `0.018` is attributable to the
innovation law with the persistence held fixed; the residual is not decomposed by this ladder. The
ladder lives in `AUDIT_R07.md` and in `R07_eta_scaling_counterfactual.csv` only, and enters neither
`docs/DEVIATIONS.md` nor any camera-ready candidate.

**The exact lattice levels differ from the two numerals v87 prints**, by `+0.053` and `+0.072`
points. Those numerals belong to R08 and R07 registers nothing on them; see the hand-off note.

Preamble §S6 declares this class. The panel titles are bold and prefixed `(A)` and `(B)`; the
shaded band is drawn at the two **exact** attainable levels rather than at the two Monte-Carlo
