# R07 — Whitening under an estimated conditional mean

R07 regenerates v87 Figure 7 and L302–L308, evaluating whitening when the conditional mean requires statistical estimation rather than perfect knowledge. Six architectural arms — `NAIVE` (zero baseline), `ORACLE` (true parameter), and four rolling-OLS windows with `n ∈ {125, 250, 500, 1000}` — are assessed over `φ ∈ {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.125, 0.15}` using 10 000 paired AR(1)-GARCH(1,1) trajectories at horizon 5 000. The exact 2δ lattice law fixes `λ* = 11.4` via absorbing-chain dynamic programming, validated against exhaustive enumeration of all `2^H` paths at `H ∈ {8, 10, 12}`.

## Execution

The pipeline executes via `./run_experiment_R07.sh` under strict 128-bit cryptographic seeding. Every stochastic task is keyed on its semantic role and index alone, ensuring mathematically paired comparisons across the φ grid. By construction, the `ORACLE` arm is bit-identical across all seven grid points, producing a flat reference curve. Control C6 verifies byte-identical artifacts across consecutive runs, and parallel execution via `--n-jobs` preserves determinism through fixed chunk boundaries.
