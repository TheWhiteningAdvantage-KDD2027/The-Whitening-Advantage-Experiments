## R02b — IID ARM Mechanism Resolution

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target

R02b extends the R02 Ljung-Box whiteness validation to a dedicated i.i.d. mechanism test, varying Student's t degrees of freedom nu across {5, 6, 7, 8.5, 12, 30} to locate the finite fourth moment boundary where the chi-square approximation fails. For Student's t innovations, E[eps^4] < inf requires nu > 4. The experiment tests 1000 streams of 8000 steps each at lag 20, measuring rejection rates and Wilson 95% confidence intervals to identify where the nominal 5% level is excluded, establishing that standard whiteness tests fail under heavy tails.

### Execution & Verification

The pipeline enforces strict S7 determinism with single-threaded BLAS, MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE, and PYTHONHASHSEED=42. All 5 test suites pass: heavy-tail arms (nu=5, 6) exclude the nominal level via test_heavy_tail_arms_exclude_nominal; nu=7 contains the nominal level per test_nu_seven_is_indistinguishable_from_nominal; negative controls hold across all nu confirming raw innovation calibration; rate ordering shows heavier tails produce higher rejection rates; and squared-raw agreement at light tails is verified. Wilson CIs use z=1.96 with design effect deff=1.

### Output Artifacts

Generated artifacts include 2 CSV files (R02b_streams.csv with 6000 rows, R02b_rejection_vs_nu.csv with 6 rows), 1 PNG figure (figA01_iid_overrejection_vs_nu.png), and R02b_claims.tex with 23 LaTeX macros defining streams per point, horizon, lags, rejection rates per nu, Wilson bounds, and nominal exclusion boundary. D2 deviation documented in DEVIATIONS.md: nu=7 rejection shifts from 9.2% to 5.8% due to BLAS threading, with qualitative mechanism preserved.
