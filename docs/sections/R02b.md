# R02b — I.I.D. Arm Resolution

R02b resolves the unresolved i.i.d.-arm claim from v87 by sweeping Student-t degrees of freedom at 1000 streams per grid point (n = 8000, lag-20 Ljung-Box). The original claim reported 9.2% over-rejection at t₇ with only 120 streams, whose Wilson interval could not separate 9.2% from the nominal 5% level.

## Methodology

Six nu values {5, 6, 7, 8.5, 12, 30} are tested with identical 128-bit deterministic seeding. Each stream is evaluated twice: on squared innovations εₜ² and on raw innovations εₜ as a negative control. Single-threaded execution under PYTHONHASHSEED=42 with MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE ensures bitwise reproducibility.

## Results

Rejection rates on squared innovations: 8.8% [7.2, 10.7%] at nu=5 (D3), 7.9% [6.4, 9.7%] at nu=6 (D3), 5.8% [4.5, 7.4%] at nu=7 (D2), 6.1% [4.8, 7.8%] at nu=8.5 (D1), 4.8% [3.6, 6.3%] at nu=12 (D0), 6.0% [4.7, 7.6%] at nu=30 (D0). The negative control on raw innovations holds 5% at all points, confirming the distortion is specific to squaring. The transition sits between nu=6 and nu=7.

## Deviation Summary

The manuscript's mechanism (t₇ lacking E[eps⁴]) is incorrect: E[eps⁴] < ∞ for nu > 4, which holds at all grid points. The squared series εₜ² has variance 2ν²/(ν-4) for nu > 4, satisfying Ljung-Box conditions throughout. The transition is not identified; the measured effect is real but at heavier tails (nu < 7) than stated. The 9.2% printed value is not reproduced; it was an ordinary draw under the true 5.8% rate.
