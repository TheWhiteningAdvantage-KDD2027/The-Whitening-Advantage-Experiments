# R04 — Iso-FPR Race and Relative Efficiency (Figure 4, Table 3)

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target

R04 demonstrates the Whitening Advantage under controlled location drifts. Four monitors race at matched 5% false-alarm rate: Recalib (standardized squared residual), Eco-L1 (QMLE-standardized location), Oracle_Eco (parametric ideal with true GARCH parameters), and Concept (binary sign-error stream). Proposition prop:orthogonality predicts Recalib is blind to location shifts (second-order entry) while Concept remains sensitive (first-order). Proposition prop:are predicts the Concept/Eco-L1 delay ratio equals 1/(4f_z(0)^2), inverting below nu* = 4.7 for Student-t innovations.

### Execution & Verification

The campaign generates 2000 GARCH(1,1) null streams of length 5000 with 500-step warm-up. Four drift magnitudes c in {0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0} and four Gamma values in {1, 11.58, 50, 200} span the design. Bisection to 5% FPR (tolerance 0.003, 15 iterations) calibrates each arm. The race Gamma of 11.58 matches SPY persistence. All artifacts pass pytest verification; the R04_claims.tex macros match the emitted CSV values through the Table 3 printing rule (3 significant figures, floored at integer precision).
