# R05 — Scale law and location/scale orthogonality (Figure 5, Appendix B)

R05 assesses Proposition prop:add_garch (linear ADD scaling in Gamma), Proposition prop:orthogonality (Concept monitor blindness to pure scale pathology), and Theorem thm:scaling (two-regime law for gradual ramps) across two controlled experiments. The Data statistic e_t = (eps_t^2 - mu_hat)/sig_hat is monitored under thirteen abrupt penalties Gamma in [1, 30] and five ramp penalties at H = 2e5 and 3e6, with 400 seeds per configuration under t_7 innovations and Delta_mu_max = 2.

## Theoretical Anchor

The experiment rests on three formal results: (i) one-sided CUSUM under abrupt scale drift yields ADD linear in Gamma at fixed Delta_mu_max; (ii) the sign stream is invariant under positive variance scaling, making Concept blindness an algebraic identity; (iii) for gradual ramps, Theorem thm:scaling predicts ADD ~ sqrt(2 lambda w / Delta_mu_max) + rho w with no fitted constant. A positive control at Gamma = 11.58 with pure location shift c = 1.0 demonstrates instrument responsiveness (400/400 detections, Fisher p < 1e-200).

## Empirical Methodology

All streams employ common-random-numbers design: 128-bit seed digests keyed on role and replicate index only. Each Data arm calibrates to 5% false alarms via its own null quantile on a disjoint hold-out half. Two crossover widths coexist: w_star_predicted from lambda_iid x Gamma, and w_delta_applied at the detector's actual threshold; both are emitted and fits use w_delta_applied as v87 prints. The lambda_iid horizon ladder regenerates thresholds at H = 77000, 200000, 3000000 from one nested trajectory set.

## Methodological Scope

Twenty-seven published quantities are classified: seven D1 and twenty D2, no D3. Wilson 95% confidence intervals accompany all detection rate and FPR comparisons. The recalibration margin degrades with horizon from [-7.4%, +29.4%] at H = 20000 to [-29.3%, +12.9%] at H = 80000. Sixth-moment boundary Gamma = 7.0793 and margin delta = 0.7931 are exact closed-form results. Panel titles are bold and left-aligned per S6.
