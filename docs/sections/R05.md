# R05 — Scale Law and Location/Scale Orthogonality

## Design
R05 validates the GARCH penalty scaling law and location/scale orthogonality across three campaigns. The abrupt shift sweeps 13 Gamma values from 1 to 30, the ramp campaigns at H = 200,000 and H = 3,000,000 verify Theorem thm:scaling with gradual scale pathologies, and the ladder campaign computes lambda_iid at three horizons for cross-validation.

## Findings
All tests corroborate the manuscript claims: detection delay grows linearly in Gamma (Proposition prop:add_garch), location/scale orthogonality holds by construction via sign stream invariance, and the recalibration margin widens with horizon from [-1.4%, +39.3%] at 2e5 to [+15.6%, +96.4%] at 3e6.

## Certification
Deviations are classified D1-D2 at printed precision due to 128-bit seeding replacing 32-bit truncation. No qualitative claims are falsified; positive controls confirm instrument responsiveness and the sixth-moment boundary Gamma = 7.1 is reproduced analytically.
