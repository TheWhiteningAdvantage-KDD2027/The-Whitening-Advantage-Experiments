### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target

R03 quantifies the false positive rate explosion when drift detectors calibrated under i.i.d. assumptions are run on stationary GARCH(1,1) streams under H_0. The mathematical target is the Siegmund-type bound exp(-2 delta_P lambda / sigma_LR^2) where sigma_LR^2 equals the GARCH penalty factor Gamma, establishing that CUSUM thresholds must scale by lambda x Gamma to maintain nominal levels, while ADWIN cut thresholds must scale by epsilon_cut x sqrt(Gamma). The GARCH penalty factor is computed in closed form from (alpha, beta) as gamma = max(1, 1 + 2*rho_1/(1 - phi)).

### Execution & Verification

The pipeline enforces strict determinism via enforce_strict_determinism() before any numerical library import, with PYTHONHASHSEED=42 pinned by the shell. Single-threaded BLAS/MKL is guaranteed through OMP_NUM_THREADS=1, MKL_NUM_THREADS=1, OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1, and MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE. Three protocols evaluate StrictCUSUM and ADWIN detectors across 300 streams of 5000 steps at 20 Gamma grid points. Aggregate certification gates verify mean FPR_raw >= 76% over Gamma > 20, mean FPR_sqrt within [25%, 35%], and mean FPR_recalib <= 13%. All CSV artifacts use float_format='%.17g' via save_fair_csv.
