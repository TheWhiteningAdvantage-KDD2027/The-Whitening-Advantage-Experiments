# R03 — False positive rate explosion without recalibration (Figure 3)

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target
R03 quantifies the cost of ignoring the heteroscedastic penalty factor Γ on drift monitors calibrated under i.i.d. assumptions when deployed on stationary GARCH(1,1) streams under H₀. Two detector classes are evaluated: StrictCUSUM with threshold λ×Γ (Siegmund-type correction for long-run variance σ_LR²=Γ) and ADWIN-like with ε_cut×√Γ correction. The protocol spans 300 streams of 5000 steps over a 20-point Γ grid from 1.17 to 200, with α=0.08 and β numerically solved for each target Γ via closed-form inversion.

### Execution & Verification
Single-threaded determinism enforced via `enforce_strict_determinism()` with 128-bit MD5-derived seeds. All aggregate certification gates pass with wide margins: mean FPR_raw over Γ>20 is 80.7% (threshold ≥76%), mean FPR_sqrt is 29.8% (within [25%, 35%]), and mean FPR_recalib is 9.6% (≤13%). Structural deterministic identities FPR_gamma≤FPR_sqrt≤FPR_raw and FPR_recalib≤FPR_raw hold row-wise with zero nesting violations over 6000 streams. Monotonicity of FPR_raw in Γ beyond Γ=6 is verified (Spearman ρ=0.9974, p<10⁻²⁰). The dedicated i.i.d. calibration arm at Γ=1 exactly measures StrictCUSUM FPR=2.0% (Wilson 95% [0.9%, 4.3%]) and ADWIN FPR=5.0% (Wilson 95% [3.1%, 8.1%]).
