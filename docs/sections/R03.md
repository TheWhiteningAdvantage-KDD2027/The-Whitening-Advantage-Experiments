## R03 — False Positive Rate Explosion Without Recalibration

### Stream Overview & Mathematical Target
R03 quantifies the heteroscedastic penalty Γ inflicted on drift detectors calibrated under i.i.d. assumptions when run on stationary GARCH(1,1) streams under H0. Target: demonstrate FPR explosion without recalibration, validate Siegmund-limit correction (λ×Γ) for StrictCUSUM, and ε_cut×√Γ correction for ADWIN. Uses 300 streams of length 5000 with α=0.08, λ_iid=65.0, δ_P=0.5, and Γ grid from 1.17 to 200.0 across 20 points.

### Execution & Verification
Pipeline enforces strict S7 determinism with single-threaded BLAS and PYTHONHASHSEED=42. All 9 tests pass: 20-point grid cardinality, Gamma grid identity against witness, structural threshold ordering (FPR_gamma ≤ FPR_sqrt ≤ FPR_raw), monotonicity beyond Γ=6, aggregate certification gates (mean FPR_raw ≥ 76%, mean FPR_sqrt ∈ [25%, 35%], mean FPR_recalib ≤ 13%), Gamma rule holds nominal 5% level, i.i.d. calibration arm well-formedness, macro emission completeness. D2 deviation documented in DEVIATIONS.md due to BLAS threading effects on GARCH path generation.
