# R10 — Sensitivity to conditional asymmetry

R10 measures `articleB_whitening_v87.tex` Figure 10 (`fig:skew_robustness`) and the third stress
test of L290: what conditional asymmetry does to the whitened sign stream. Fernández–Steel skew-`t`
innovations on `ξ ∈ {1.00, 0.85, 0.65, 0.50}`, **1 000 paired streams per point**, horizon
`n = 8 000`, three CUSUM calibration arms — `half` (`ref = 1/2`), `oracle` (`ref = q_oracle`) and
`qhat` (`ref = q̂` over a 1 000-step warm-up).

**The paragraph's qualitative content reproduces in full.** The two Ljung–Box arms stay within
`4.6–6.3 %` of the `5 %` nominal across the whole `ξ` grid while the fixed-`1/2` false-alarm rate
climbs monotonically from `0.5 %` to **`96.6 %`**, and recentring on the warm-up estimate holds the
rate below nominal everywhere (`1.0–1.5 %`). One printed numeral moves at v87's own printing
precision — the realized skewness, `−1.44 → −1.43` — and one caption numeral does, the upper end of
the measured FPR envelope, `1.8 % → 1.5 %`. Both are the mandated re-keying and both are **D2**.

**Two facts about this figure are not in its caption, and they change how it reads.** Panel A's
raw-sign curve is i.i.d. `Bernoulli(q)` *by construction of the delivered code* — `ε_t = √h_t · z_t`
with `h_t > 0`, so `1{ε_t > 0} = 1{z_t > 0}` bit-exactly on all 4 000 streams — so that curve
measures the Ljung–Box test's calibration and not a property of the data-generating process; the
classifier-error curve is the stress test. And panel B's three arms are drawn against a `5 %`
nominal line **this detector cannot reach**: at `δ = 0.1` and `λ = 15.0` the level it delivers under
perfect centring is `0.345 %`. Both are reported here and in `docs/audits/AUDIT_R10.md`; neither
opens a register entry, because the caption's statements are true and it prints no nominal level for
this detector.

## The measurement

**The design is paired, and that is a design choice with a price.** The entropy plan preamble §S6
mandates keys the stream on its **index alone**, so the same `|T|` and `u` draws serve all four `ξ`.
Every cross-`ξ` comparison is therefore paired by construction, and no pooled interval may be read
before the design effect is measured (control C9).

| `1.00` | `−0.00023`        | `0.499485` | `4.7 %`   | `5.0 %`   | `0.5 %`    | `0.4 %`      | `1.0 %`    |

**The `ξ = 1` witness is not centred on `1/2`, and the reason is deterministic.**
`get_fs_moments(7.0, ξ)` fixes the standardisation constants by a 10⁶-draw `RandomState(42)`
Monte-Carlo. At `ξ = 1` the Fernández–Steel **density** is symmetric, but the constant it returns is
`m = 8.4786 × 10⁻⁴`, which is not zero. Two things have to be said together about it:

1. **It is within one standard error of zero.** A 10⁶-draw mean of a variable whose standard
   deviation is `1.1838` carries a standard error near `1.2 × 10⁻³`, and `|m|` is `0.716` of it.
2. **It is a fixed deterministic constant of the apparatus.** It is drawn once and subtracted from
   every stream of every run, so it displaces `q` by the same amount every time and never averages
   away.

Saying only the first makes the shift look like sampling noise; saying only the second makes the
standardisation look broken. The stream `z = (Y_raw − m)/s` is therefore not centred on zero, and
the sign stream is not centred on `1/2`: its centre is `q* = P(Y_raw > m) = 0.4996736`, the exact
Student-`t₇` survival at `m`. The measured `q = 0.499485 ± 0.000177` sits **`−1.07` standard errors
from `q*`** and **`−2.91` from `1/2`**. The R10 prompt's §2.1 — "`xi = 1` est le cas symétrique" —
is a **specification imprecision of the prompt about the stream**, recorded as such and not silently
repaired; v87 claims nothing about the `ξ = 1` cell.

Preamble §S6's sharing rule is what settles the treatment: the entropy migration applies to draws
that produce a Monte-Carlo **measurement**, while a draw that fixes a deterministic **constant of the
measuring apparatus** belongs to the experimental specification and is carried verbatim, with the
constant persisted and asserted at run time. `get_fs_moments` stays byte-identical, the triple lands
in `R10_fs_constants.csv` beside its closed form, and control C6 asserts that the triple every one of
the 4 000 workers used is bit-identical to the one the main process recomputes.

**The fixed-`1/2` arm has an exact law, and it is the law the arm delivers.** At `ref = 1/2` the two
CUSUM branches move by `+0.4` and `−0.6`, i.e. by `+2` and `−3` in units of `2δ = 0.2`, so `M_H` is
an integer multiple of `0.2` and `λ = 15.0` is exactly **75 lattice units**. An absorbing-chain
dynamic program over the joint state `(S_pos, S_neg)` therefore gives `P(M_H > λ)` exactly, at any
`q`, consuming no entropy:

| ------ | ------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `1.00` | `0.500 %`           | `0.368 %`              | `0.434 %`              | `+0.30`                                      |
| `0.85` | `4.100 %`           | `3.887 %`              | `4.357 %`              | `−0.41`                                      |

The predictions at the **measured** `q` are within `0.8` standard errors of those at `q*` at every

**The gate this design does *not* buy, stated rather than glossed.** v87's claim is that the sign
stream is i.i.d. `Bernoulli(q)` at *every* `ξ`, so Ljung–Box should be calibrated at every `ξ` and
not only at `ξ = 1`. Gating on the `ξ = 1` cell plus an invariance criterion is the **weaker** of the
two available designs and is chosen deliberately: gating on all eight cells at the `5 %` level is a
family whose trigger probability under a perfectly calibrated null is `1 − 0.95⁸ = 33.66 %`, far
above the `5 %` ceiling §S4bis fixes. The two gates adopted give `1 − 0.99² = 1.99 %` (`2.97 %` if
C2a's two arms and C2b are counted separately). **They are not presented as covering the claim
exhaustively.** The prescribed pooled KS of the eight per-cell p-values is computed and reported
(`D = 0.3617`, `p = 0.1916`) and gates nothing: the eight cells share all 1 000 streams, so the KS
null's independence assumption fails outright.

## Known deviations from the submitted manuscript

### D2 — the campaign is redrawn (`R10-campaign-redraw`)

Preamble §S6 requires a 128-bit `SeedSequence` keyed on the role and index alone; the delivered
script keyed each stream on a bare integer and built `np.random.RandomState(seed)` inside the DGP.
The migration redraws all 4 000 streams. Every Monte-Carlo value moves; two printed numerals move at
v87's own printing precision.

| v87 site           | printed   | regenerated   | z (one campaign) | z (difference of two) | severity |
| ------------------ | --------- | ------------- | ---------------- | --------------------- | -------- |
| L290 skewness      | `−1.44`   | **`−1.4280`** | `+2.76`          | `+1.95`               | **D2**   |
| L290 `q`           | `0.58`    | `0.582191`    | `+12.40`         | `+8.77`               | D1       |
| L290 `≈97 %`       | `0.97`    | `0.966`       | `−0.70`          | `−0.49`               | D1       |
| Fig. 10 FPR lower  | `1.0 %`   | `1.0 %`       | `0.00`           | `0.00`                | **D0**   |
| Fig. 10 FPR upper  | `1.8 %`   | **`1.5 %`**   | `−0.98`          | `−0.69`               | **D2**   |

The printed values are themselves single Monte-Carlo realisations of the same design, so the second
`z` — through the standard error of the *difference* between two campaigns — is what a comparison of
two campaigns supports; the first is what classifies the numeral. Camera-ready candidates
`R10_v87_L290_skewness_numeral.md` and `R10_v87_caption_fpr_envelope.md` propose the two edits.
Pre-classified Class A / D2 by the `R11-regenerated`, `R05-campaign-redraw`, `R13-campaign-redraw`
and `R07-campaign-redraw` precedents.

The `q` numeral's `+12.40` deserves one sentence: `0.58` is printed to two decimals and the
regenerated `0.582191` rounds to `0.58`, so no printed value moves. The large `z` reflects a standard
error of `1.8 × 10⁻⁴` on a mean of 8 × 10⁶ binary draws, not a disagreement — both campaigns place
the marginal rate where the standardisation constant puts it, `q* = 0.582348`, from which the
regenerated mean is `−0.89` standard errors.

## Reported and not registered

**Panel A's raw-sign curve is i.i.d. by construction of the delivered code.** `ε_t = √h_t · z_t` with
`h_t ≥ ω = 8 × 10⁻⁴ > 0` by induction, so `1{ε_t > 0} = 1{z_t > 0}` bit-exactly — asserted by control
C4 on all 4 000 streams, 0 disagreements. That curve therefore measures the Ljung–Box test's own
calibration; the classifier-error curve is the stress test. **No register entry**: the caption's
sentence is true and the perimeter filter keeps an incomplete-but-not-false formulation out of the
register. The camera-ready candidate `R10_v87_panelA_sign_arm_scope.md` proposes the clarifying
clause.

**The HT-error arm's evidence is a non-rejection, and its power is already registered elsewhere.**
`docs/DEVIATIONS.md` `R18-ljungbox-power` covers L290 and fixes the lag-1 autocorrelation the test
resolves with probability `0.8` at `n = 8 000`, lag 20 — R10's exact configuration. **R10 opens no
duplicate entry** and cross-references R18. Its own contribution is the full per-stream p-value
distribution in `R10_skew_streams.csv`; the smallest p-value over the 4 000 streams is
`2.62 × 10⁻⁴` on the HT-error arm and `3.77 × 10⁻⁴` on the raw-sign arm.

**`fpr_qhat` is a factor of 1.6–3.8 above `fpr_oracle`, and C8 fixes the level either can reach.**
The recentred arm spans `1.0–1.5 %` and the oracle arm `0.4–0.9 %` (per-`ξ` ratios `2.50`, `1.56`,
`2.20`, `3.75`), against an operator floor of `0.345 %`. v87 prints no nominal level attached to this detector — a grep of the frozen `.tex` for a
printed nominal at `δ = 0.1`, `λ = 15.0`, `n = 8 000` returns nothing; L241's `λ* = 11.4`, L266's
`λ_C* ∈ [10.6, 10.7]` and L270's `λ_C = 10` are other detectors at other horizons — so **no register
entry is opened** and the measurement is reported here and in `docs/audits/AUDIT_R10.md`. Control C8
is an exploratory measurement needed by a control, which the perimeter filter keeps out of both the
register and the camera-ready candidates.

**The exact fair-coin level of this operator is `0.3677 %` at `λ = 15.0`**, with `0.4334 %` at
`λ = 14.8` and `0.3120 %` at `λ = 15.2`. Those numerals appear nowhere in v87 and R10 registers
nothing on them; they are in `R10_lattice_exact_law.csv` for whoever needs the operator's own scale.

## Cosmetic divergence from the submitted figure

Preamble §S6 declares this class. The panel titles are bold, prefixed `(A)` and `(B)` and centred
(the delivered script writes `'A. Autocorrelation Robustness (Whitening)'` at the matplotlib default
location); both panels carry a grid; and **panel B carries a second reference line**, the level
control C8 measures this operator to deliver under perfect centring, which the submitted figure does
not draw. No numerical value moves on any of these accounts.
