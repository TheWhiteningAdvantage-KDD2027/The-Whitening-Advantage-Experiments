## Deviation Classification

### D2 Deviation: Floor Fraction Envelope
**ID:** R16-floor-frac-envelope

v87 L329 states: "even there the floor consumes 55--92% of the phase." The compliant pipeline measures [50.1%, 92.1%] over the 13 phases the ceiling does not exclude at gamma = 20 unconditional. The upper end reproduces; the lower end does not. The single phase at 54.8% rounds to 55%, which suggests the published lower bound was read off that one phase rather than off the minimum of the set. Cause not identified.

**Affected Macros:**
- `\RSixteenFloorFracMin`: v87 prints 55%, compliant measures 50.1%
- `\RSixteenSpyLongFloorFrac`: v87 implies 55%, compliant measures 54.8%

### D2 Deviation: Sharpe One Cost Evaluations  
**ID:** R16-sharpe-one-cost

v87 L260 prints: "at the permissive gamma = 20 ... a drift of SR = 1 costs at least ~1,510 trading days; at the operational gamma = 252 ..., ~2,790." The compliant pipeline evaluates these as 1509.85 and 2786.83 respectively.

**Affected Macros:**
- `\RSixteenSharpeOneCostGammaTwenty`: v87 prints ~1510, compliant measures 1509.85
- `\RSixteenSharpeOneCostGammaTwoFiftyTwo`: v87 prints ~2790, compliant measures 2786.83

### D2 Deviation: COVID Floor Values
**ID:** R16-covid-floor-values

v87 L331 prints: "a floor of ~34 trading days at one false alarm per year, and 18.5 days at the loosest calibration." The compliant pipeline measures 34.12 and 18.49 respectively.

**Affected Macros:**
- `\RSixteenCovidFloorGammaTwoFiftyTwo`: v87 prints ~34, compliant measures 34.12
- `\RSixteenCovidFloorGammaTwenty`: v87 prints 18.5, compliant measures 18.49

### D1 Deviation: Out of Budget Fraction
**ID:** R16-out-of-budget-frac

v87 prints 80% as an integer. The compliant pipeline measures 80.3%. At printed precision (integer percentage), this is invariant.

**Affected Macros:**
- `\RSixteenOutOfBudgetFracGammaTwenty`: v87 prints 80%, compliant measures 80.3%
